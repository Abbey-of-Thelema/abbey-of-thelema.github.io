The Dark Knight Rises: Meaning and Symbolism

Antichrist's decode of The Dark Knight Rises: Bane explains the pit before he puts anyone in it, and why hope makes it the worst hell.

THE DECODE, AS POSTED

Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.

Bane explains the hole before he puts anybody in it.

There is a reason this prison is the worst hell on earth. Hope. Men look up at the light and imagine climbing to it, and like shipwrecked men turning to seawater, many have died trying. There can be no true despair without hope. So he will feed a city hope to poison it.

Then the film shows you a rope.

A strong man climbs. The house chants. He reaches the ledge. Twelve feet of air. He jumps. The rope catches him. He slams the rock. They lower him, bruised, breathing, no closer than the morning. Every prisoner wears the cord. Every prisoner fails the same jump. Falling with a rope on is not falling. It is an inconvenience. The protection took the death out of the drop, and the drop is the only door.

The light at the top of the shaft is not an escape. It is the sea coming in through a crack. The cord is how you keep drinking it. Hope is not the opposite of the prison. Hope is the plumbing.

The whole film is in that one object. The film knows it. It hangs the same rope in front of the same man three times.

Read the three titles as one sentence. Batman Begins. The Dark Knight. The Dark Knight Rises. Something arrives. Something reigns in the dark. Something comes back up. The third name is Horus. It is never the man who died. Keep that in the corner of the eye.

Picture first. Name second. If I reverse that, stop reading. You can check this against a film you have already seen and books that cost nothing.

Three things you will be able to explain. Why Bane is not the villain. Why the rope is why nobody escapes. Why the ending is not sad.

The last film ended with two acts and a lie. A man in a mask took blame he did not earn. A commissioner stood in front of a city and said nothing true. An old line names four powers of the Sphinx: to know, to will, to dare, to keep silent. Two were spent on that rooftop. To dare: he asked to be hunted. To keep silent: Gordon smashed a signal and carried a name. This film takes the lie back and spends the other two in a hole.

The tomb

Wayne Manor is a mausoleum with a living man in it.

Cane. Locked wing. Eight years. No enemy in the house. No woman in the house. Alfred sets a place and then stops setting it. Then he walks.

He is buried before the plot starts. Nobody did this to him.

He does want something. People get that wrong when they call him empty. He wants to be useful to the city his father built. That is an inherited will. Losing the company is not a money problem. It is the will cut off at the root. The machine in the basement is the father’s work in a new casing. He shelved it himself.

Alfred has already described the other ending. Florence. A café on the Arno. Fernet Branca. A table he would not speak across. He says he never wanted him to come back to Gotham. He says it in a kitchen while the tomb is still pretending to be a house. Remember that table.

There is Bruce Wayne. There is Batman. And there is a third man who has been holding both in one body since he was a boy in a well. Nobody names him. What happens in the next two hours happens to him.

The thief

A maid comes for a tray and leaves with his mother’s pearls.

She takes them off a safe he was told could not be opened, in his own house, while he watches. She lifts his prints. She kisses him. She is out the window before the conversation is over.

The first film opened with that necklace breaking in an alley and scattering into the dark. Here a woman who wants her past deleted takes the string off a case.

Later she wears the pearls in a room full of people who think they own the weather. There is a storm coming, Mr. Wayne. She is not wrong. She is not yet the point.

That is the first hand. It belongs to a thief. It is the only one of the four that will still be there at the end.

To keep silent

Gordon has a page in his pocket he cannot give.

He wrote the truth about Harvey Dent years ago. Every year since, he has stood at a podium and read something else. The lie holds men in prison. It holds the city’s law. He carries the real page like a stone.

He does not get to confess it. It gets taken. A man in a mask stands in a stadium and reads that page in Gordon’s own words.

That is the power spent on the rooftop being pulled back off the man who was holding it. To keep silent. Assigned. Then revoked. In public.

The station running this film gives its virtue as Silence. Its weapon, on the same tables, is the Outer Robe of Concealment. Not a disguise. A garment you put on to do the work.

Four robes in the first movement. A mask that cannot come off a face. A mask in a glass case with dust on it. A woman whose face is the mask. A thief who wears whatever the room requires and drops it when it stops paying.

The largest robe is not on a person. Eight million people are wearing Harvey Dent. A city built its quiet streets on a story about a man who put a gun in a child’s face. It holds. It works. It is a lie the whole time.

A king may choose his garment as he will. There is no certain test. A beggar cannot hide his poverty. Gotham chose. Underneath it is still a beggar. The moment the page is read, everyone can see it.

Belief is not hope

Alfred watches a man fight in a hole and does not say muscle.

Look at him. His speed. His ferocity. His training. I see the power of belief. I see the League of Shadows resurgent.

That is a man naming the exact thing his own boy no longer has.

Hold the words apart.

Belief is a man who does what he says. Bane says what he will do, does all of it, dies doing it, and does not lie once.

Hope is what he pours on eight million people to keep them sitting still. A bomb with a timer they can see. Anyone who leaves will be the reason it goes early.

Belief moves a man. Hope holds a city in place. They are not the same word. The film keeps them in separate hands.

Two titles, one letter

Two women. One presents as a thief. One presents as a benefactor. The film does not tell you which is which until it has to.

The tables give this station two names a letter apart. Ama. The dark sterile mother. Aima. The bright pregnant mother. Same word. The only difference is whether anything has been conceived.

One was born in a hole. She carries her father’s line. What she delivers is not a child. It is a device with a five-month term and a date announced in advance. She wears another woman’s name until the veil drops. She says you have to invest to restore balance to the world. That is the house speaking. It sounds like charity.

The other wants her record erased. Not forgiven. Unwritten, so a future is possible. Fox even builds her the program that eats a life off the books. She sells him in a stairwell. She comes back. At the end she is wearing the pearls that broke in the alley, on a living neck.

Nuit is not a costume. She is the field. Infinite space. The condition that lets anything happen. The film puts the sky over the bay and leaves her standing at a table in the sun. I am not going to do anything else with that here.

If you are waiting to tell the women apart by looking, you cannot. Neither can the book. A neophyte takes the road of evil. Samael says return, thou canst not pass by. He takes the road of good. Metatron says the same sentence. Two angels. Same office. Looking fails. That is why the first watch trusts the wrong woman.

Saturn

Watch what the first movement takes off him.

A fortune, on an exchange floor, with his own fingerprints. A body, in a sewer, in a few seconds, with a sound the film makes sure you hear. A future, on paper. Then a clock over the city for five months, counting in public.

Money, body, time. That looks like three beats. It is one attribute. The tables refer this station’s darkness to Saturn. Lead. Limit. The clock. The reaper. Time as a wall.

The vice on the same line is Avarice. A businessman wants the company. His fixer wants the fortune. A floor of men want the numbers to move. They are the first to die. The man who kills them does it himself and walks out with nothing in his hands.

One more entry on that line, printed next to the robe. BABALON. I am not going to explain it here. Keep it in the corner with Horus. Notice where the water goes.

The blow

He fights him in his own cave. His own floor. His own craft scattered under them.

Both men came out of the same school. One adopted the dark. One was born in it. One trained and left. One was thrown out and kept the doctrine. Victory has defeated you. When Gotham is ashes, you have my permission to die. No one cared who I was until I put on the mask.

Look at what he attacks. Not the mask he could have killed in the dark. Not the rich man he had already ruined that morning. He takes money, back, and city in one operation. He is not after either half. He is taking apart the man in the middle who has been holding both since the well. Break that joint and both halves fall without being touched.

They do. For five months.

Without that adversary the buried one stays in the wing with a cane. Nobody else was coming. Alfred quit. The company folded. The police could not find him. The man who broke his back is why he is alive at the end. The film does not ask you to forgive him. It asks you to notice the job.

To know

A blind man in a hole looks at a broken back and says the back is not the problem.

You do not fear death. You think this makes you strong. It makes you weak. How can you move faster than possible, fight longer than possible, without the most powerful impulse of the spirit. The fear of death.

Sit with it. Two films trained you to admire the burning off of fear. He went up a mountain and had it drilled out. He has walked into rooms nobody else would enter. A doctor with no eyes calls all of that work the illness.

Not the spine. Not the wall. The courage.

That is to know. A man told what is actually wrong with him, unable to talk it off.

The old manual describes what the higher attendant does when it arrives. Comfort is not on the list. Blessings. Offenses. Where you erred in the Operation. Diagnosis, then instruction.

The blind man is running that office. He is not selling anything. He is not afraid of Bruce Wayne. He is the only figure in three films who tells him what is actually wrong.

The empty father

The well comes back.

First film: a boy at the bottom, a father on a rope, why do we fall, so we can learn to pick ourselves up. Warmest line in the trilogy.

This film: the same shape. A hole. A rope hanging.

Nobody comes down.

The first rope had a father on it. This one has nobody. What has to go is not safety. It is the expectation that anybody is coming.

Falling with a rope on is not falling. The prisoners are not weak. They are safe. No stakes, no fear. No fear, nothing to jump with.

The congregation

They chant while he climbs. He asks what it means. A prisoner says rise.

That is the only assistance he accepts. Not a hand. Not a harness. Voices from men who failed the same jump and are still wearing the same cord.

Someone offers the rope again. He says no.

To will

The first two tries still have the rope on. The body is willing. The drop is not real. The third try is the one the doctor described.

He jumps. Bats come out of the wall. The same bats. The same well. This hole and that hole were always one hole.

That is to will.

He does fear something. It arrives when the cord comes off. I fear dying in here while my city burns, and there is no one there to save it. Not pain. The work unfinished. The only fear a man with a real will can have. Nobody gave it to him. They stopped protecting him from it.

Two powers were left on the rooftop. They get spent on this wall. Neither is announced until you look back.

The child who climbed

The internet still has this backwards.

The only person who made that climb was a child too small to tie. No rope, because none would fit. That child was Talia.

Bane stayed. The prisoners tore him apart so she would have the gap.

The man running the ordeal already paid for somebody else’s climb with his body. Now he puts a rich man in the same hole, takes the same cord, and calls it mercy.

He did the ordeal. She did the jump.

The pair

Abramelin, in Mathers’ introduction, states it as doctrine.

To each man is attached from birth both a Guardian Angel and a Malevolent Demon, and it rests with him which is given the victory. Attached. Not summoned. Not earned. Both standing. The variable is authorship.

The same book will not let you reverse the order. Knowledge of the higher first. Only then any right to use the lower. He fails at everything until the pit. He succeeds at what comes after.

Notice which is louder. The demon has hands in the room. Door. Floor. Back. Microphone. The other does not kick. A pocket. A disappearance. A late return. Still standing at the end.

The strong one is not the prize. The strong one is the door.

The city as the hole

While he is in the pit, the city is in the pit.

A man on a plane is told he is about to become useful. Thirty seconds later he is dead. It happens to the doctor. The businessman. The men who carried Bane. Everyone who serves the purpose stops, including his own. He takes nothing for himself.

He seals the police under the streets. Hands the city its corruption. Opens the prisons. Tells eight million they are free to choose. Same operation two films ran on individuals. Scale is the difference. Consent is the objection. Nobody asked Gotham what it most desired.

Stadium. A child still on the grass. We take Gotham from the corrupt. The rich. Myths of opportunity. Gotham is yours. That is seawater in a microphone. The myth of opportunity is the rope at city scale. Then he reads the page. The robe comes off in public. Five months begin.

Foley hides in a basement and will not die for a city that does not care. The tomb, written small. Crane calls exile justice on ice. Special forces hang from a bridge.

Two equal forces produce immobility. The negation of life. Motion only when one side outweighs the other. Last film: two boats, two detonators, a vote that could not pick up a trigger, one convict who could. This film: a winter, a clock, a city standing still. It breaks the same way. Not a vote. One preponderance.

The hands

Count them. A thief on the pearls. A friend handing him every instrument. A benefactor’s hand, and that is the one that ends him. Four people reach. Only one is taking something.

The shot that matters: two hands on the machine in the floor. A star in a cage. Nuit is the field named when the women were named. Hadit is the point that moves inside it. The film does not lecture the names. It puts hands on metal and makes the metal work.

What comes out has a five-month term. It is not a child. It is carried out over the bay and goes into the water.

This station is also called the Great Sea. The bomb goes into the sea. The name can pass because the man holding it has already written himself out.

Sorrow

The tables give this station one more entry. The Vision of Sorrow. Not triumph. Sorrow, as what is available at that height. Myrrh on the same line.

The only unambiguously happy scene in three films is an old man crying at a café table in Florence.

He looks up. Across the room, a man with no mask, a woman with pearls on a living neck. No speech. The kitchen fantasy, paid at a distance. He weeps.

The officers

Two handovers.

He fixed the autopilot months before the flight and let everyone he knew believe he had not. He wrote his own death in advance and walked out of the story alive. To dare, on the rooftop, was asking another man to hang him. To dare, here, is paperwork.

A young cop has been here the whole time. Coat on an orphan. A boys’ home Bruce paid for and forgot. No such thing as coincidence anymore. That is a candidate being told the terms.

A clerk asks his name. He says Robin. A cave opens. Lights in a hole under a house.

A boy finishes an anthem. The ground opens in every direction and does not touch him. Later a man tells an orphan a hero can be anyone.

Two children, if you want to be exact, and one office. The boy on the grass is the picture. The cop at the desk walks through the door. Do not mash them until a rewatch makes you.

The first film left a man at a door and another man arriving at it. Hold onto that. This is what for. The one who kept the post takes the mask off and leaves. The one who can no longer believe in coincidence walks in. Same doorway. Opposite jobs. You get to watch it.

On the old tables the sun is three pictures at once. A majestic king. A child. A crucified god. This trilogy spent the king on a prosecutor a city tried to keep clean. It spent the dead god on a man who asked to be hunted. The child was the picture nobody picked up.

He does not get to watch the boy grow up. That is what the ending is. It is not sad. It is just not his anymore.

The books are free.

Crowley’s 777 is the table. Line 3: Ama and Aima, Silence, the Outer Robe, Saturn, the Great Sea, Avarice, the Vision of Sorrow. Abramelin, Mathers, the introduction that names both attendants. Konx Om Pax, the two angels who say return. Liber AL, chapter two, the garment. Liber Libræ, the four powers, and unbalanced force if you want the five months in one line.

I am not smarter than you. I read books. That is the difference.

Watch it again. Tell me what I missed.

Who is the child in the last Matrix, the last Rings, the last Wars.

Love is the law, love under will.
