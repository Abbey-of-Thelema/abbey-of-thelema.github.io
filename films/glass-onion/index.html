My take: a metaphorical ascent from Malkuth’s material trap to truth, blending Johnson’s social commentary with occult vibes. The Beatles’ ‘Glass Onion’ title nods to illusions: “Looking through the bent-back tulips” echoes the elite’s warped view. Miles’ island as false paradise—crossing “waters” via invite, no phones for forced isolation. Enter Benoit Blanc: guides Helen collaboratively (not alone—teamwork theme), handing strategy and “courage” for her ascent. Climax: destroys the loaned Mona Lisa, triggering Klear explosion—symbolic fall of Miles’ empire, “releasing” truth publicly for accountability.

No pure forgiveness, but ironic mercy through justice: Helen turns betrayers into allies via self-preservation, transmuting chaos. Antichrist energy?
