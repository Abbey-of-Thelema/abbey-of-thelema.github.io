Inside the dome of the United States Capitol, 180 feet above the Rotunda floor, is Constantino Brumidi’s fresco The Apotheosis of Washington, painted in 1865. It shows George Washington rising into the heavens, with groups from Roman myth around the edge of the canopy: Minerva, Neptune, Venus, Mercury, Vulcan and Ceres (Architect of the Capitol). Photographs of the dome show a ring of five-pointed stars around the central group; that ring is not mentioned on the AOC page, and this is the house’s own observation. It is commonly counted as 72, and videos online call the number occult and the stars pentagrams.

The Architect of the Capitol’s page on the fresco does not mention the stars or give a star count, and the house has not counted them, so the number 72 is unverified here.
