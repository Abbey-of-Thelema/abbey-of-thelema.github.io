🚨AntiChrist Decodes:
WHEN A MAN LOVES A WOMAN. ‼️

They play it at weddings.
It's about a man getting played.

Route: Malkuth → Path 32 (Tav) → Yesod → Path 28 (Tzaddi) → Netzach. He never reaches Tiphareth, the sixth room.
The Tree of Life is a very old map: ten rooms, the climb off the ground.
777 is Crowley's book of tables. Every row is a room or a road on that map.

Percy Sledge sang it like a man on his knees.
That is why nobody reads the words.

🌐 MALKUTH — Adonai Melekh
777 Key 10. The Kingdom. Ten of Disks (coins): Wealth.

Lyrics
“Spend his very last dime”
“turn his back on his best friend”
“He’d give up all his comforts”

PROFANE
A man going broke over a girl.

INITIATE
Pull up the card. Count the coins.
Last coin. Last friend. Last roof.
By verse two the floor is paid out.

⚡ PATH 32 (TAV) — The Universe
777 Key 32. The World. Last card in the deck, first door off the floor.

Lyrics
“He’d trade the world / For the good thing he’s found”

PROFANE
He would give up everything.

INITIATE
Third line of the song.
Pull up the card. A woman dancing in a wreath.
He spends the last card first.

🌙 YESOD — Levanah
777 Key 9. The Foundation. Sphere of the Moon.

Lyrics
“Can’t keep his mind on nothin’ else”
“If she is bad, he can’t see it”
“Loving eyes can never see”
“He’s the last one to know”

PROFANE
Love is blind.

INITIATE
Four times the song says the eyes are wrong.
He is looking at the picture he made, not the dancer.

⭐ PATH 28 (TZADDI) — The Star
777 Key 28. Letter list: The Husband. The Star is the Gate of the Sanctuary.

Lyrics
“And sleep out in the rain / If she said that’s the way it ought to be”

PROFANE
He will do what she says.

INITIATE
Pull up The Star. A figure under the night sky, pouring water.
Outside. Water falling. Because she spoke.
Not his will. Hers.

💚 NETZACH — Nogah
777 Key 7. Victory. Sphere of Venus. Seven of Cups: Illusionary Success.
Column L: Unselfishness.

Lyrics
“When a man loves a woman”
“She can bring him such misery”
“I give you everything I’ve got”
“And baby baby please don’t treat me bad”

PROFANE
A love song. Then everybody cuts the cake.

INITIATE
Venus is love. He lands in her room.
Pull up the Seven of Cups. A man staring at treasure in a cloud.
One of the cups is holding a wreath.
That is the good thing he found.
He named The World. He is standing in this card.

In Crowley’s fairy tale The Wake World (Konx Om Pax, 1907) a girl is walked up this same map.
In the Seventh House she marries and nearly sleeps.
“Most people who reach the Seventh House stay there all their lives.”

The song says baby.
The old books put the Child in the sixth room, the Sun’s room.
He calls that room from here. Nobody answers.

He sings at her the whole song.
She never says a word.
No voice. No fire. No angel.
An Angel cannot play him. She can.

Unselfishness is the virtue of this room.
He has it perfected.
Look what it bought him.

🔄 RETURN TO MALKUTH

Lyrics
“If she is playing him for a fool”
“I know exactly how he feels”

PROFANE
The singer steps in.

INITIATE
The man describing the path was already on it.
The song says fool.
777 on the Fool: Folly’s Doom is Ruin.
Ruin is the Ten of Swords.

Every Ten is the floor.
He left it as Wealth.
He comes back as Ruin.
Same row in the book.
He paid the full price and bought Netzach, and the floor still has him.

What remains visible
A last dime on the floor.
The last card spent as the first door.
Baby called. The sixth room does not answer.
A title that never leaves the room.
Eyes that cannot tell the wreath from the cloud.

You’ve been singing the I for years.
I missed it too.
So who’s the last one to know?

He has the love. Whose will is it under?

Love is the law, love under will.
