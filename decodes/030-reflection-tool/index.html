🔮 ANTICHRIST DECODE #30 — Reflection, Tool

Eleven minutes of a man crying in the dark?

Then what secret did the moon just tell him?

93 93/93

666 – Abaddon

Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.

(Thelema is Greek for "will": the one thing you're here to do, not whatever you feel like.)

For years I heard this as a sad song. It's a man walking from a hole in the ground to the sun.

You don't need to know any of this. I'll explain every word as we go. Pull up the cards I name, then play the song again.

Route: Malkuth → the Moon road → Netzach → the Death road → Tiphareth → home.

THE FLOOR. Malkuth (Hebrew, "kingdom"), the bottom of the map.

He starts "curiously close to the end," down in a "pitiful hole." Then: "It's calling me." Pull up the Judgement card. The dead are standing up out of their graves because an angel is blowing a trumpet. A man in a hole, and a call from above. But he doesn't climb out yet. In his "darkest moment," the next voice he hears is the moon's.

THE MOON ROAD. From Malkuth up to Netzach.

"The moon tells me a secret." Then the secret: "As full and bright as I am, this light is not my own." Hear it as the moon talking. A full moon is the brightest thing in the night, and none of that light is hers. Waite, writing about the Moon card: "The intellectual light is a reflection." There's your title. Now pull up Crowley's Moon card. Under the water, a beetle carries the sun in its jaws. The sun never left. It's underneath.

"As I pull my head out." This road is path 29, the Hebrew letter Qoph, and in Crowley's tables Qoph means "back of head." He pulls his head out on the road named for the head, like the crayfish at the bottom of Waite's Moon card, "crawling from the abyss of water to the land."

The Moon road ends at Netzach (Hebrew, "victory"). Getting his head above water is a win. He doesn't stop at winning.

THE DEATH ROAD. Path 24, from Netzach into Tiphareth. Its card is Death, "Lord of the Gate of Death."

"Crucify the ego." The song says ego, not body. Nobody gets hurt. The old self dies so the real one can stand up.

"I pray the light lifts me out." Pull up Waite's Death card. Waite: "a prelate with clasped hands awaits his end." And on the horizon, "there shines the sun of immortality." The light he prays to is painted on the card.

THE SUN. Tiphareth (Hebrew, "beauty"), the center of the map.

Count the suns. The one under the water on the Moon card. The one on the horizon of the Death card. And the one he sang about back on the Moon road, the "source" that's "bright and endless." It's the same sun every time, the one the moon was borrowing from, and here he finally stands in it. Crowley's 777 lists this sphere's power as "The Vision of the Harmony of Things (also the Mysteries of the Crucifixion)." Both halves are in the song: "crucify the ego," and then "we are all one mind."

HOME. Back down to the floor.

At the end he isn't alone in the hole anymore. He's talking to you: "let the light touch you." The first time, he sings "Before I pine away." The last time, it's "Before we pine away." He went to the Sun and came back for you.

I'm not smarter than you. I just had the cards out while the song played, and the song kept pointing at them.

So whose light have you been calling your own?

Love is the law, love under will.

666 – Antichrist
