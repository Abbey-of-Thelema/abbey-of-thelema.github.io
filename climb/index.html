Welcome

Thelema is the Greek word for will. It is the law of The Book of the Law, which Aleister Crowley wrote down in Cairo on 8, 9 and 10 April 1904. Its code is short: “Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.” “Love is the law, love under will.” “Every man and every woman is a star.”

Source: Liber AL vel Legis (The Book of the Law), with Crowley’s Introduction (Introduction, part III; chapter I, verses 3, 40 and 57).

Aleister Crowley (1875–1947) was an English occultist, poet, novelist and mountaineer, and the founder of Thelema. He also made 777, the table of correspondences this site uses to read the Tree.

Sources: the Wikipedia article “Aleister Crowley” (copy on file); the title page of 777 Revised.

The Tree of Life is ten spheres joined by twenty-two paths. 777 counts them together as “32 paths, of 10 numbers and 22 letters”: each path carries a Hebrew letter and a tarot trump.

Sources: 777 Revised, “On the Magical Alphabet”, p. 132; Dion Fortune, The Mystical Qabalah, chapter IV.

The climb. Malkuth, the Kingdom, is the bottom sphere: the world as we live in it. Dion Fortune calls it the lowest point of the outward road, “through which all life must pass before returning whence it came”, and she writes that the way back is a serpent coiling upward on the paths, “the symbol of initiation”. This site is laid out the same way: you start here and go up one door at a time.

Source: Dion Fortune, The Mystical Qabalah (first published 1935), chapter XXV, “Malkuth”, §1 and §22. The last sentence is the house’s own description of this site.
