PROPOSED A-HAs (he rules)

  1. Rain is already Fire of Water. At [L15] she "set fire to the rain". 777, the sub-elements of the Triplicities: "Fire of Water. d Rain, Springs, etc.—swift passionate attack." (l.2410). The title's two enemies are one thing in the book; she sets the fire on what is already fire in the water. Sources: 777 l.2410, and l.2402 for the neighbouring row. No lens needed.
  2. One gate card, two faces. L2–L4: "you rose... you saved me" is the Last Judgement's rising (Waite l.1979–1981). L36: "threw us into the flames" is the same card's other face: "the destruction of the world by Fire" (Thoth l.4022–4023). 777's one-line Shin: "Resurrection is hidden in Death." (l.2329). The song is raised by Judgement and burned by Judgement. Rests on the gate being 31; if he picks another, this falls.
  3. Kiss, heart, rain, fall: Nuit's chapter. Liber AL II:62 and II:67 hold the kisses, the heart, the rain and the swoon: "the kisses of the stars rain hard upon thy body" (l.614), "Hold! Hold! Bear up in thy rapture; fall not in swoon of the excellent kisses!" (l.631). The song's kiss (L4), weak knees (L5–L8), heart (L1) and rain (L15) come in the same order of act: kiss, fall.
