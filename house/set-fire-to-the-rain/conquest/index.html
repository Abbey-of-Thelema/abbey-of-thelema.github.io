KIND

  • One voice. Adele (the "I") sings to a "you" who rose to claim her fallen heart, kissed her and saved her, then is found to have a side she never knew: what he said was never true, and the games he played he always won. She sets fire to the rain, watches it pour on his face, and it burns while she cries. She has lain with him and called it better. After she throws "us" into the flames, something dies (two copies: she felt it die). She still wakes by the door and looks for him, though they are already over. She ends by telling it to burn.
  • HIS LENS (one artist singing to a higher version of himself) does not fit this "you" cleanly. A higher self is a hard match for "the things you'd say, they were never true" and "the games you'd play, you would always win." I read it as one woman singing to a lover. If he wants the higher-self reading, it has to live with that untrue, game-winning "you"; I don't force it.
  • What happens, first line to last: a fall of the heart, a rise and a saving kiss in the dark, a standing that is already a fall to the feet, the hidden side, the fire put to the rain, a remembered lying-together, the same fire, a death or a felt death and "the last time," then the waking world at a door, a caught heart still waiting, and "let it burn."

THE TREE

a. Pictures. Card images aren't on the box, so all picture matches are unverified against the pictures themselves. I held each picture the song paints against the house lists as pointers, then opened Waite (and Thoth for the gate) only for the cards those lists, or the song's own words, pointed to. I did not read Waite for all 78 cards.

  • A heart that falls, is claimed, and later is a "heart you caught," with rain and crying. This points to Swords Three: "Three swords piercing a heart; cloud and rain behind" (Waite l.2705). Also to Ace of Cups, "House of the true heart" and, reversed, "House of the false heart" (l.2569–2571).
  • Setting fire to the rain; flames; "let it burn"; it burned while she cried. This points to XX Judgement / Shin / Fire, and to XIV Temperance / Art (fire and water together), and to XVI The Tower (flame and ruin). See e and the verses.
  • A kiss that saves, lying together, "you and me together." This points to the Lovers as an idea of a pair, but I did not find Waite's Lovers picture (Adam and Eve, the angel, the trees) in so many words in this song, so I leave the card off the sheet rather than force it. Cups Two ("A youth and maiden are pledging one another… Love, passion, friendship, affinity, union," Waite l.2550–2555) is the same kind of pair; WEAK, listed below.
  • Waking by a door, and waiting there. The door as a word is Daleth in 777, not a Waite picture I opened. Thomas 75 is the text for the door (see verses). I found no Waite card whose opened text is "a door" in so many words among the cards I opened.
  • Dark, at the start. Moon, divinatory: "darkness, terror, deception" (Waite l.2976–2977). The deception ("never true") fits; "terrified" is not a word this song sings. Pointer only unless used below.
  • Falling to the feet, a figure down. Tower has figures who suffer a fall (Waite l.1892–1893); Swords Ten is "A prostrate figure… also pain, affliction, tears" (l.2630–2632). Both are possible pictures; the Tower is stronger once the flames are in the later chorus. See e.
  • A name screamed, a last time, a rise from a fall. Judgement: an angel's trumpet, the dead rising. See e and h.

b. Numbers. The song sings no number. "The last time," "never knew," and "always win" are not numbers. "21" is the album title, not a word in the lyric. Nothing from Col. CLXXVI.

  • Water: rain, pour, cried. 777, row 23, Mem, "Water" (l.491). Row 4, "Snow, rain, spirit of life, blessings" (l.1578), and "God the Rain-make" (l.913), which is Chesed/Jupiter, not a path I put on the road unless the song gets there; I don't force it.
  • Air: a kiss (HIS RULES, read widely), and "screaming" as a voice. Thin for a card. Nothing I would defend as a path by itself.
  • Earth: a door as a threshold, feet, standing. Thin except as Daleth's word, which is already a letter, not an element claim.

d. The lens.

  • The call: not a second singer. She hears "it" (the fire-in-rain) "screaming out your name." That is a name called out of the element, not a second recorded voice. I note it and do not call it a duet.
  • The Moon: "It was dark"; later she "wake[s] up." Deception is in the lyric ("never true"). The Moon is a second-gate candidate, not my pick. See e.
  • Saturn or time: "forever," "the last time," "already over."
  • Angels, wings, religious words: none in the lyric. Judgement's angel is in the book, not in the song's words.
  • Breath and silence: "Close my eyes." Thin.
  • Lines that sound like harm, read as symbol: "I threw us into the flames," "let it burn," "something died" / "I felt something die." I read them as the end of the union (the death of that "us"), not as a literal act, and I do not cut them.

VERSE BY VERSE

Verse 1: "I let it fall, my heart / And as it fell, you rose to claim it"

  • Tree: gate 31, the dead rising. Waite l.1979–1980, "the dead are rising from their tombs"; 777 l.2210–2211, "The dead arising from their tombs." The heart falls; "you rose to claim it" and "you saved me." MATCH, and I propose the gate itself as an A-HA with the fire lines below, not this rise alone.
  • "I let it fall, my heart" and later rain and crying: Swords Three, "Three swords piercing a heart; cloud and rain behind… division, rupture" (Waite l.2705–2707). MATCH.
  • "It was dark": the second-gate case for 29, as in e. Not my pick.
  • "you kissed my lips and you saved me": the saving belongs to the Judgement rise. The kiss itself I do not hang a card on.

Pre-chorus: "there's a side to you / That I never knew" / "never true" / "always win"

  • Thomas 5, l.112: "Know what's in front of your face, and what's hidden from you will be revealed to you, because there's nothing hidden that won't be revealed." She later "touched your face" and has already said there is a hidden side. MATCH for hidden-and-revealed, not for the face as a pointer by itself.
  • Ace of Cups, reversed: "House of the false heart" (Waite l.2571), against "the things you'd say, they were never true." MATCH for the reversed meaning only. Upright, "House of the true heart" (l.2569), is contrast.

Chorus: "But I set fire to the rain / Watched it pour as I touched your face / Well, it burned while I cried"

  • Tree: Shin as Fire, and Thoth XX, "Shin, which refers to Fire. The card therefore represented the destruction of the world by Fire" (l.4022–4023). STRONG for the fire; proposed as an A-HA together with the opening rise, because the same card holds both the rising and the fire.
  • Thomas 10, l.128: "I've cast fire on the world, and look, I'm watching over it until it blazes." She sets fire, and she "watched it pour," and she ends "Let it burn." MATCH, and I propose it as an A-HA. Anyone who has heard Thomas 10 will hear "watched it" after "set fire" differently.
  • Thoth, Art l.3502: "The fire burns up the water; the water extinguishes the fire." MATCH for the title act. Waite's Temperance, which "tempers, combines and harmonizes" (l.1831–1832), and Thoth's "Fire and Water harmoniously mingled" (l.3515): contrast. She burns the rain; she does not harmonize it.
  • "screaming out your name": I do not grade this as Judgement's trumpet. The book has a trumpet; the song has a scream from "it." The picture is not the same act. Left out of the line; too thin for MATCH.

Bridge: "When I lay with you… You and me together"

  • Thomas 61, l.311 and 315: "Two will rest on a couch. One will die, the other will live." And, "if they're divided, they'll be full of darkness." She lays with him, then (two copies) feels something die, and the song began "It was dark." MATCH. I do not rest this on AZ's "When it fell, something died" (one copy).
  • Thomas 11, l.131: "On the day when you were one, you became divided. But when you become divided, what will you do?" She sings "You and me together" and then throws "us" into the flames. MATCH, as the question the later chorus answers.

Later chorus: "I threw us into the flames" / "the last time"

  • Tower, path 27, as in e: "the ruin of the House of Life" and "a House of Falsehood" (Waite l.1879–1881). MATCH, a road from Hod, not the gate.
  • "the last time" with Judgement's name and "outcome" (Waite l.2983): idea, already carried by the gate. No extra grade.

Bridge 2: "Sometimes I wake up by the door / That heart you caught must be waiting for you"

  • 777, Daleth, "Door" (l.482). STRONG: the source says the letter is Door, in so many words, and the song sings "door."
  • Thomas 75, l.374: "Many are waiting at the door, but those who are one will enter the bridal chamber." She wakes by the door; "that heart you caught must be waiting for you"; they are "already over," not one. MATCH, and I propose it as an A-HA. The waiting is at the door; the entry is for "those who are one," which they no longer are.
  • Thomas 59, l.301: "Look for the Living One while you're still alive. If you die and then try to look for him, you won't be able to." She looks after "something" has died and they are over. Near, but the "Living One" is not her "you" in so many words. WEAK, listed below.
  • This is the waking world in the lyric. I did not see a door in the official stills.

Outro: "Let it burn"

  • Returns to Shin's fire and to Thomas 10's "until it blazes." No new source.

PROPOSED A-HAs (he rules)

  1. Gate 31, Shin, Judgement: the opening rise ("you rose to claim it," "you saved me") and the title fire are the same card. 777 and Thoth say Shin is Fire and connect it to the fire of the Last Judgement; Waite and 777 have the dead rising. I would say this to his face with the song and those pages open.
  2. Thomas 10: "I've cast fire on the world, and look, I'm watching over it until it blazes" against "I set fire to the rain / Watched it pour" and "Let it burn."
