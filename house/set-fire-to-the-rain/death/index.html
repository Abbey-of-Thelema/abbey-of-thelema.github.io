7. PROPOSED A-HAs. He rules.

  1. "I set fire to the rain" is 777's own element pair. 777.txt l.2410 lists "Fire of Water. d Rain, Springs, etc.—swift passionate attack." The title act is that element, and 1 Kings 18:38 has fire licking up water. Needs his eye on whether the sub-element table reads as I read it.
  2. L40 and Thomas 75. "Many are waiting at the door, but those who are one will enter the bridal chamber." Her heart is waiting at the door (L40-41), once "together" (L23) and now divided (Thomas 11). Graded MATCH, not STRONG.
  3. The Three of Swords. Waite: "Three swords piercing a heart; cloud and rain behind." The song burns the rain behind the pierced heart (L1, L15-17). The card is on Binah, Understanding, Saturn (THE TABLE, the four Threes). Card picture is unverified (no image on the box). This one rests on Waite's words for the card, not a picture.
