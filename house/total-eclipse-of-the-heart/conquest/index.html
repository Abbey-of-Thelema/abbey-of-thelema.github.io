KIND

  • Two voices. Bonnie Tyler (the "I") sings to a "you" who is turned away. A second, male voice (Rory Dodd) breaks in before each line with "Turn around" and "Turn around, bright eyes". On this recording the call comes from outside her voice. HIS LENS (one artist singing to a higher self) fits only in part. The refrain is a second person calling, and I don't read the two as one voice.
  • What happens: loneliness, then tears, then years gone, then terror, and each time the look in "your eyes" steadies her. Then she falls apart. In the bridge she wants to hold on forever, but "your love is like a shadow on me". At the peak it's an eclipse: "Once upon a time there was light in my life / But now there's only love in the dark."
  • Story around it: Steinman told Playbill he "actually wrote that to be a vampire love song. Its original title was 'Vampires in Love'... It's all about the darkness, the power of darkness and love's place in the dark..." (Wikipedia, citing Playbill). Tyler told Record Mirror it is about "someone who wants to love so badly she's lying there in complete darkness." Meat Loaf said it was written for him, and Tyler and Steinman both denied it (Wikipedia).
  • Video: unwatched (the box can't play video). From Wikipedia's account, Russell Mulcahy filmed it at Holloway Sanatorium, a Gothic hospital. Tyler is in white, dreaming of boys at a boarding school, with a choir.
  • Cover: not seen. Wikipedia names a "Picture sleeve of UK and some European releases" but doesn't describe it.

VERSE BY VERSE

Intro and verse 1: "Turn around / Every now and then I get a little bit lonely"

  • Tree: gate 29, Qoph, "the back of the head" (Thoth l.3917; 777 l.478). The song opens with a second voice asking someone to turn, so she faces the back of his head. MATCH, and I propose it as an A-HA. Anyone who has heard Qoph is the back of the head won't hear "turn around" the same way again.
  • "the sound of my tears": the Moon's dew as the "tears of _Isis_" (Waite l.685-686). MATCH. Her tears fall the way the Moon card's dew falls.
  • "I get a little bit terrified": the Moon's "darkness, terror" (Waite divinatory, l.2976). Also Thoth, "the black towers of nameless mystery, of horror and of fear" (l.3938). STRONG.
  • "And then I see the look in your eyes": Thomas 25, "Protect them like the pupil of your eye", is too thin and goes to WEAK. Thomas 37 fits better: "then [you'll] see the Son of the Living One and won't be afraid." The terror ends when she sees. MATCH.

Pre-chorus: "Turn around, bright eyes / Every now and then I fall apart"

  • Thomas 61: "if they're divided, they'll be full of darkness". Falling apart and then living in the dark is the order this song follows. MATCH.

Verse 2: "I dream of something wild" / "lying like a child in your arms"

  • Tree: 777 on Qoph, "the fluctuating craving of the animal soul" (l.5446-5447). "Restless", "something wild", every now and then. MATCH.
  • Thomas 22: "Jesus saw some little children nursing... 'These nursing children can be compared to those who enter the kingdom.'" She lies like a child in the arms. MATCH, but contrast in one way: Thomas's children are entering, and she is helpless.

Bridge: "Your love is like a shadow on me all of the time / I'm always in the dark / We're living in a powder keg and giving off sparks"

  • Tree: road 27, the Tower. "A tower struck by forked lightning" (777, row 27 picture, l.2203). "unforeseen catastrophe" (Waite l.2968). Sparks and a powder keg are Fire about to burst. STRONG.
  • "Forever's gonna start tonight": Thomas 51, "What you're looking for has already come, but you don't know it." Contrast: she puts forever off until tonight.

Chorus: "Once upon a time there was light in my life / But now there's only love in the dark /... A total eclipse of the heart"

  • Tree: Tiphareth is the heart (777 l.2769), and its heaven is the Sun (THE TABLE). A total eclipse of the heart is Tiphareth's Sun hidden behind the Moon, seen from the Moon card's path. STRONG on 777's words, and I propose it as an A-HA.
  • Thoth XVIII: the Beetle "bears the Sun in his Silence through the darkness of Night" (l.3924-3925), and the Moon "is the poisoned darkness which is the condition of the rebirth of light" (l.3935-3936). The hidden Sun is carried, not lost. MATCH.
  • Thomas 28: "they were blind in their hearts and couldn't see." STRONG, since an eclipse of the heart is a blind heart. Thomas 24: "Light exists within a person of light... If they don't shine, there's darkness." MATCH with "light in my life / only love in the dark". On the Qoph road itself, 777 sees two "squat turrets, blind as the fool's heart" (l.5451). STRONG: a blind heart on the gate road.
  • "Nothing I can say": Peh, "Mouth" (777 l.476), on road 27. Contrast: the road's letter is a mouth, and she has no words.

Verse 3: "no one in the universe / As magical and wondrous as you"

  • "universe": 777's name for the World (32, Tau). WEAK, a shared word only.

Outro: "Turn around, bright eyes / Turn around"

  • It ends on the call, unanswered. The song closes where it opened, at Qoph. That's my reading.

PROPOSED A-HAs (he rules)

  1. "Turn around" is Qoph, "the back of the head" (Book of Thoth XVIII; 777). The whole song is sung to the back of a head, at the gate of the Moon.
  2. A "total eclipse of the heart" is Tiphareth's Sun covered by the Moon, and 777 marks the Qoph road with turrets "blind as the fool's heart". 777 says "Tiphareth the heart", and the Moon card "represents midnight", with Khephra carrying the hidden Sun.

WEAK (ten at most)

  • Nine of Swords for lying in the dark: she's seated in the card, not lying.
  • Road 21, the Wheel, for "every now and then".
  • "universe" and Tau.
  • "the end of the line" as Earth.
  • Thomas 25, "pupil of your eye", for "bright eyes".
  • Cheth row 18 "Vampires" (777 l.2228). It's backstory only, and off the road.
  • Teth row 19, "Heart" and "Horror" (777 l.2229). Same row, same thin tie.

DROPPED, AND WHY

  • Ayin, "Eye", for "bright eyes". It's on the Devil (6-8), which the song never reaches, and a shared word is no more than that.
  • Gate 32, the World. "the best of all the years have gone by" is time (Saturn), but there's no picture or idea of the World's dance. Too thin.
  • Cups Five as loss: the song has no cups, and the loss already sits in the Swords.
  • Thomas 87 and 112, on body depending on body ("I need you more than ever"). It's a moral reading the song doesn't make.
