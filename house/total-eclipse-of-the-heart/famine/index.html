KIND

  • Power ballad, about 7:02 on the album Faster Than the Speed of Night (1983); the single is about 4:30 (Wikipedia raw, famine-src/wiki.txt l.37-73).
  • Two voices. Tyler sings the verses. Rory Dodd sings the "(Turn around)" calls (wiki.txt l.47, credits l.117). "Turn around, bright eyes" comes from Steinman's 1969 musical The Dream Engine (l.49). So this is a call and an answer, not one voice.
  • The writer says what it is about: its first title was "Vampires in Love", written for a Nosferatu musical; Steinman: "the power of darkness and love's place in the dark" (Playbill, quoted in wiki.txt). Tyler: someone "lying there in complete darkness" (Record Mirror, quoted there). These are the makers' words, not mine.
  • Video: unwatched (Russell Mulcahy, Holloway Sanatorium, per Wikipedia only).
  • Lens: the "you" is mostly a human lover ("the boy", l.68). The lens of a higher self fits "no one in the universe / as magical and wondrous" (l.74-75), but it does not fit "never be the boy you always wanted to be". I mark it where it fits and nowhere else.

THE TREE a–h

a. GATE: 29, Qoph, the Moon (Malkuth to Netzach). An eclipse is the Sun hidden in the dark. Thoth says this card "represents midnight", and the beetle "bears the Sun in his Silence through the darkness of Night" (Thoth l.3920-3925). 777: "the Sun at midnight… ATU XVIII", Pisces "that greatest darkness before the dawn" (l.4694-4697). The song: "always in the dark" (l.48), "only love in the dark" (l.60), tears (l.8), terror (l.14), a dream (l.23). The other gate weighed: 31, Judgement (a voice calls; the "Turn around" call is sung by a second voice). It is weaker, because nothing rises and nothing is judged.
b. ROAD: from Netzach, 27, Peh, the Tower (Netzach to Hod). "Fall apart" (l.17 and 7 more times), "falling apart" (l.55), "a powder keg… giving off sparks" (l.49). Thoth: "the destruction of existing material by fire… Falling from the tower are broken figures" (l.3788-3796). Waite: "ruin" (l.1869). Peh means "Mouth" (777 l.476), and the song says "Nothing I can say" (l.61): the mouth fails. Weighed and not taken: 24, Death (the vampire story fits "forever's gonna start tonight", but the lyric never names death), and 28 and 21 (nothing in the lyric).
c. WHERE IT STOPS: at Hod, below the Sun. "There was light in my life / now there's only love in the dark" (l.59-60). The song never gets to the Sun (path 30) or Tiphareth, and ends on the call (l.113-114). The dawn is promised by the cards (Thoth: "the poisoned darkness which is the condition of the rebirth of light", l.3935-3936), not by the song.
d. NUMBERS: only the count-in "Three, four" (copy 1, l.1, one copy only). Col. CLXXVI: Gimel = 3, Daleth = 4 (777 l.2140-2141). It's a band count and in one copy only, so it goes in WEAK.
e. ELEMENTS: Water (tears l.8, cry l.29), Fire (sparks, powder keg l.49), Earth ("end of the line" l.46), no Air. Thoth on this path: "The moon has no air" (l.3941). The Air is missing in the song too: MATCH, my reading.
f. TIME: "every now and then" (l.4 on), "tonight" (l.39, l.51), "forever" (l.42, l.51), "once upon a time" (l.54). Midnight is the Moon's hour (Thoth l.3920).
g. THINGS AND BODY: "heart". 777 Col. CLXXXII row 19 (Teth, Leo) is "Heart", and Col. CLXXXIII row 19 is "Horror, Dragons" (l.2229). Leo is the Sun's sign, so an eclipse of the heart is the Sun hidden in its own part of the body. The 777 rows are fact; the reading is mine. "Eyes" (l.15, l.16): 777 l.475 gives Ayin = Eye (path 26), but that path is not on this road, so it's WEAK. Col. CLXXXIII row 18 is "Vampires" (l.2228); that comes from Steinman's story, not the lyric, so it's WEAK.
h. THE CALL: "Turn around" (l.3 and on). Qoph means "Back of head" (777 l.478; Thoth l.3917). A call to turn around speaks to the back of the head. See the PROPOSED A-HAs.

VERSE BY VERSE

  • V1 (l.3-11), "a little bit lonely… tired… nervous": the dark of the gate begins. Tears (l.8) are Water, Pisces. MATCH.
  • Chorus 1 (l.13-19), "terrified", "the look in your eyes", "fall apart": terror on the Moon path (Waite: "the fears of the natural mind", l.1933), then the falling of the Tower. STRONG.
  • V2 (l.21-29), "dream of something wild": Waite: "life of the imagination apart from life of the spirit" (l.1931). MATCH. "Lying like a child" (l.26): Waite's Sun is "the heart of a child", but that card is off this road, so it's WEAK. Thomas 46: "whoever… will become a little child will know the kingdom", WEAK.
  • Bridge (l.39-52), "the end of the line", "a shadow on me all of the time", "always in the dark", "powder keg", "forever's gonna start tonight". Liber AL II:9: "all the sorrows are but as shadows; they pass & are done" (l.460). The song says the shadow is there "all of the time"; AL says it passes. CONTRAST, MATCH.
  • Refrain (l.54-62), "falling in love / now I'm only falling apart", "light in my life / only love in the dark". Thomas 61: "if they're divided, they'll be full of darkness" (Thomas file l.315). Falling apart, then darkness: STRONG. Thomas 24: "If they don't shine, there's darkness" (l.186). MATCH.
  • V3 (l.67-79), "no one in the universe": the word Universe only (path 32, Tav). WEAK. The lens fits here (see KIND).
  • End (l.111-114): it ends on the call, unanswered.

PROPOSED A-HAs (mine, for the check)

  1. "Turn around" against Qoph, the Back of the Head (777 l.478; Thoth l.3917). The second voice keeps asking her to turn around, on the very path whose letter means the back of the head. Thoth: the letter "is connected with the potencies of the cerebellum". The gate is from (a); the turning is my reading.
  2. "A total eclipse of the heart": the Heart is row 19, Leo (777 l.2229), the Sun's sign, on the same row as "Horror". It is the Sun hidden in its own body part, on a Moon gate that "represents midnight". The 777 rows are fact; the joining is mine.
