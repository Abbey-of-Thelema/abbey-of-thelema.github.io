KIND

  • Two voices. Bonnie Tyler sings the verses and choruses. Rory Dodd sings the "Turn around" and "Turn around, bright eyes" call (Wikipedia, read 01:52 AM MT). So it is a duet in form, a call and an answer. HIS LENS warning applies: this is not one voice singing to a higher self; the "Turn around" voice is a second voice calling to her.
  • To whom: she sings to "you", a lover who is "never coming around". In verse 3 she names him: "the boy".
  • What happens: she lists her moods "every now and then", lonely, tired, nervous, terrified, then sees "the look in your eyes" and falls apart. The chorus begs him to hold her, "Forever's gonna start tonight". Then the turn: "Once upon a time I was falling in love / But now I'm only falling apart", and "Once upon a time there was light in my life / But now there's only love in the dark". It ends where it began, with the call "Turn around".
  • Stories: Steinman told Playbill "Its original title was 'Vampires in Love' because I was working on a musical of Nosferatu... It's all about the darkness, the power of darkness and love's place in the dark" (Wikipedia). Genius: the line "Turn around, bright eyes" came from Steinman's "Come in the Night" in his 1969 musical The Dream Engine. Tyler on Genius: "Lots of people seem to think the song is inspired by vampires... I've always interpreted it as an impassioned love song." Some Genius listeners read voices in the head into "Turn around" (rumor, listeners' reading).
  • Video: unwatched. Account used: Wikipedia, which says Russell Mulcahy directed it at Holloway Sanatorium, Tyler in white, dreaming of boarding-school boys, a choir.
  • Cover: not seen; nothing read about it.

e. The gate and the roads.
Two gates fit the opening. My pick is 31 Shin, Judgement (777: Angel or Last Judgement), Fire, joins 8-10, to Hod.

  • For 31: the first words of the song are a call, "Turn around", sung by a second voice, and it calls through the whole first third. Waite's Judgment is a summons "heard and answered from within" (l.1987 to 1988), and in Waite's card the child's "back is turned" (l.1981). The song's call is to one who is turned away.
  • For 29 Qoph, Moon, Pisces, joins 7-10, to Netzach: tears, fear, "terrified", dreams of "something wild", and the dark. Waite's Moon is "the fears of the natural mind" (l.1933). 777's letter meaning for Qoph is "Back of head" (l.479); "Turn around" asks the back of the head to turn.
  • I pick 31, because the call is spoken in so many words and the Moon is only implied. He chooses.

Roads from 8 Hod (copied from THE TABLE):

  • 26 Ayin, Devil, Capricorn, 6-8: "bright eyes", "the look in your eyes". 777: Ayin "Eye" (l.475). This road leads to 6 Tiphareth, which 777 calls the heart (below).

Where it stops: at 6 Tiphareth. 777: "Tiphareth the heart" (note to Col. CIII, l.2768 to 2769). The heart is the place the song names, and it is eclipsed. My reading: the path that leaves Tiphareth upward on the Middle Pillar is 13 Gimel, High Priestess, Moon, 1-6 (THE TABLE). A solar eclipse is the Moon in front of the Sun. So the song stops at the heart, with the Moon in front of it, and does not reach path 13 or the One.

VERSE BY VERSE

Verse 1, "Turn around / Every now and then I get a little bit lonely"

  • Gate 31, Judgement. Waite: "between them their child, whose back is turned" (The Last Judgment, l.1981) and "the summons of the Supernal--which summons is heard and answered from within" (l.1987 to 1988).   • Thomas 38: "There will be days when you'll look for me, but you won't be able to find me." (Mattison, l.229). MATCH. "You are never coming around" is the same act: looking for one who doesn't come.
  • Gate 29 alternative, Qoph. 777: Qoph "Back of head" (l.479). MATCH. The call is to turn the back of the head around; shown under e as the second gate.

Verse 1, "Every now and then I get a little bit terrified / then I see the look in your eyes"

  • The Moon. Waite: "Hidden enemies, danger, calumny, darkness, terror" (l.2976). MATCH. Terror and the dark are the Moon's own words, and she is "terrified".

Verse 2, "I'm lying like a child in your arms"

  • 777, row 6: "Phœnix, Lion, Child" (l.971). Tiphareth's animal is the child. MATCH, mine: the child she becomes in his arms is the heart's own figure.

Bridge, "Once upon a time I was falling in love / But now I'm only falling apart"

  • Thomas 11: "On the day when you were one, you became divided." (Mattison, l.131). MATCH. Falling in love into one, then falling apart, is one becoming divided.
  • Thomas 61: "if someone is /equal\, they'll be full of light; but if they're divided, they'll be full of darkness." (Mattison, l.315; Mattison's note l.511 says "equal" is a one-letter emendation of "destroyed"). STRONG, set with the next line: divided, then dark, is the bridge in so many words.
  • Three of Swords. Waite: "Three swords piercing a heart"; "division, rupture" (l.2705 to 2706). MATCH.

Bridge, "Once upon a time there was light in my life / But now there's only love in the dark... A total eclipse of the heart"

  • Stop at 6 Tiphareth. 777: "Tiphareth the heart" (note to Col. CIII, l.2768 to 2769). With THE TABLE: Tiphareth's heaven is Sun; path 13 above it is Gimel, Moon. A-HA (proposed), my reading. An eclipse of the heart, on the Tree, is the Moon covering Tiphareth, the Sun and the heart, so the climb stops there.
  • Thomas 24: "Light exists within a person of light, and they light up the whole world. If they don't shine, there's darkness." (Mattison, l.186). STRONG. Light, then no light, then darkness is the line's own order.
  • Thomas 28: "they were blind in their hearts and couldn't see." (Mattison, l.198). MATCH. A heart that can't see is an eclipsed heart.
  • Road 30, Resh, Sun. Waite on the Sun: "typified by the heart of a child" (l.1960). MATCH, contrast. The Sun card is the heart's light shining; the song has lost it.

"Turn around, bright eyes" (×3, interlude)

PROPOSED A-HAs

  1. "Turn around" is XX Judgement: a second voice summons one whose back is turned, as Waite's child "whose back is turned" (l.1981) hears the summons "answered from within" (l.1987 to 1988). The call opens and closes the song.

  3. Thomas 61 and 24 together: "if they're divided, they'll be full of darkness" (l.315) and "If they don't shine, there's darkness" (l.186) say the bridge, "falling apart" then "only love in the dark", in its own order.
