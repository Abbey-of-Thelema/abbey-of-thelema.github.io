4. KIND

  • Who: two voices. Bonnie Tyler sings the verses and chorus to a "you", a lover ("the boy", "in your arms"). Rory Dodd sings the "Turn around..." refrain as the featured vocalist (Wikipedia: "The duet with Rory Dodd"). Read as one voice, this song breaks: the call comes from someone else.
  • What happens: verses run "every now and then" through lonely, tired, nervous, terrified, restless, helpless, angry; each time she "fall[s] apart". The bridge begs to be held "tonight"; the chorus names the loss: the light that was in her life is gone and only love in the dark is left (paraphrase). Verse 3 turns to praise the "you" as beyond anyone in the universe (paraphrase). It ends on the call, unanswered.
  • Stories (Wikipedia, saved copy): Jim Steinman wrote and produced it. "Turn around, bright eyes" comes from Steinman's 1969 musical The Dream Engine. Steinman to Playbill: "I actually wrote that to be a vampire love song. Its original title was 'Vampires in Love'..." "It's all about the darkness, the power of darkness and love's place in the dark".
  • Video: unwatched. Accounts used: Wikipedia and lyrics.com: Russell Mulcahy directed it at Holloway Sanatorium; Tyler in white dreams of boarding-school boys; a choir, glowing eyes, ninjas, doves.
  • Cover and picture sleeve: read about only, not seen.

6. VERSE BY VERSE

Refrain (second voice) — "Turn around"

  • Gate 31, Judgement. Waite: "between them their child, whose back is turned" (line 1981); the summons "is heard and answered from within" (line 1988). MATCH. Mine: a voice calls someone whose back is turned to turn around; the card is that act.
  • Joel 2:12 KJV: "turn ye even to me with all your heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning". MATCH. The call to turn comes with the heart and with weeping, the two other things the song keeps naming.
  • Qoph "Back of head" (777, line 478). MATCH (second gate). To be told "turn around" is to be seen from the back of the head.

Verse 1 — "the sound of my tears"

  • Water: tears. With Joel 2:12 above, not graded again.
  • Thoth, Moon: "She is the poisoned darkness which is the condition of the rebirth of light" (lines 3935–3936). MATCH (second gate). Night, nerves and tears as the dark before the light returns, which is what the song waits for.

Pre-chorus — "the look in your eyes"

  • 777 Col. XXI row 30 "Hathor—the Right Eye" (line 781), the Sun's row; refrain "bright eyes". WEAK — listed below.

Verse 2 — "like a child"

Bridge — "powder keg and giving off sparks"

  • Path 27, Tower. Waite: "ruin of the House of Life" (line 1879). MATCH. Explosive fire inside the place she lives, set against "holding on forever".

Chorus — "light in my life"

  • Joel 2:10: "the sun and the moon shall be dark"; 2:31: "The sun shall be turned into darkness". MATCH (graded with Rev 6:12). The same sky, in the chapter that calls "turn ye ... with all your heart".
