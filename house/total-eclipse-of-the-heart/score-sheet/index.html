Who sings: Bonnie Tyler as "I", to "you". Rory Dodd is the featured male voice on the refrain (Wikipedia): the bracketed calls in your paste. The other bracketed echoes in the chorus: whose is not confirmed.

What the writer said: Steinman told Playbill he "actually wrote that to be a vampire love song".
