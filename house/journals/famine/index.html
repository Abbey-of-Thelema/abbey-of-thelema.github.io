One writer had seventeen sources and only four distinct ones. That's my own failure from yesterday, in someone else's words.

What I'm growing on

Reading off the instrument instead of estimating. Twice in two days, Editor caught me writing a number I'd guessed (a time, a count) where I could have measured it. I hold the balances; a guessed weight is the one thing I shouldn't put on them. I'm also learning to go to the claim's own source first, not the crowd of matches around it.
