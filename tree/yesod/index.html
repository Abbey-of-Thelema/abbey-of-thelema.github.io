Yesod · The Foundation

The ninth sphere, the first one up from Malkuth by this site’s centre door, path 32. Every line below names its book. 777 comes first.

The books: 777 Revised, Aleister Crowley (the 1955 Neptune Press reprint of 777, 1909), in the Celephaïs Press text of 2004; and Dion Fortune, The Mystical Qabalah, first published in London in 1935 (Weiser edition, 1999 printing). § numbers are Fortune’s paragraphs. “Row” is 777’s row: row 9 is Yesod; Fortune’s chapter XXIV is Yesod.

What it is

Yesod means the Foundation. It is the ninth sphere. (777, Cols. II and III, row 9.)

Its heaven is Levanah, the Sphere of Luna, the Moon. (777, Cols. VI and VII, row 9.)

Fortune: it stands “towards the base of the Pillar of Equilibrium”. (Fortune, ch. XXIV, the table.)

Fortune: “Yesod might aptly be described as the Sphere of the Machinery of the Universe. If we liken the kingdom of earth to a great ship, then Yesod would be the engine-room.” (Fortune, ch. XXIV, §3.)

Fortune: “it is from Yesod that Malkuth receives the influx of the Divine forces.” (Fortune, ch. XXIV, §13.)

Its virtue: independence

777, in its column of Transcendental Morality, gives row 9 the word Independence. (777, Col. L, row 9.)

Fortune gives the same: Independence. (Fortune, ch. XXIV, the table.)

Its vice: 777 gives the Moon (row 13) “Contentment [Idleness]”; Fortune gives Idleness. (777, Col. L, row 13; Fortune, ch. XXIV, the table.)

Its spiritual experience: the Vision of the Machinery of the Universe

777: “The Vision of the Machinery of the Universe.” (777, Col. XLV, row 9.) Fortune gives the same. (Fortune, ch. XXIV, the table and §2.)

Fortune: the waters of chaos are “finally gathered up and organised” by the images designed in Hod, and that organising is “the Machinery of the Universe,” the vision of which is this sphere’s experience. (Fortune, ch. XXIV, §3.)

Its God-name and the rest

  • God-name: Shaddai El Chai, “Almighty and Ever-living God: refers to his function as Pangenetor.” (777, Col. V, row 9, and its note.) Fortune: “the Almighty Living God”. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Archangel: Gabriel. (777, Col. XCIX, row 9.)
  • Choir of angels: Kerubim; Fortune adds “the Strong”. (777, Col. C, row 9; Fortune, the table.)
  • Magical image: “A beautiful naked man, very strong.” (777, Col. CXXI, row 9; Fortune gives the same.)
  • Path of wisdom: the Pure or Clear Intelligence. (777, Col. XIII, row 9.) Fortune quotes its text: it “purifies the Emanations”. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Tarot: the four Nines. (777, Col. XIV, row 9; Fortune, the table.)
  • Colours, in 777’s four scales: King, indigo; Queen, violet; Emperor (Yetzirah), very dark purple; Empress, citrine flecked azure. (777, Cols. XV to XVIII, row 9.) Fortune gives the same four by world. (Fortune, the table.)
  • In the body: the reproductive organs. (Fortune, the table.)

What it means for someone climbing

Fortune: Yesod “is the first Sphere with which he makes acquaintance when he commences to ‘rise on the planes,’ and lifts consciousness above Malkuth.” He enters it by “the terrible Thirty-second Path of the Tau”. (Fortune, ch. XXIV, §14.) On this site that is the centre door at Malkuth, path 32.

Fortune warns that Yesod, “considered by itself, is unquestionably the Sphere of Illusion”, the “Treasure House of Images”, and that the keys to it “are to be found in Hod, the Sphere of Magic.” (Fortune, ch. XXIV, §14.)

Fortune: on the Western path the grades “go straight up the Central Pillar to Tiphareth”. (Fortune, ch. XXIV, §14.) On this site, the centre door at Yesod, path 25, goes up to Tiphareth.

Left out

The grade of the order for Yesod (777, Col. CXXII) is left off: the copy on file does not line the grades up with the rows clearly enough to be sure. Lines that begin “On this site”, and the words “this site’s” in the opening line, describe this site’s own doors, not a book.
