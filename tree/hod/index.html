Hod · Splendour

The eighth sphere, at the foot of the Pillar of Severity (Fortune), behind the left door at Malkuth. Every line below names its book. 777 comes first.

The books: 777 Revised, Aleister Crowley (the 1955 Neptune Press reprint of 777, 1909), in the Celephaïs Press text of 2004; and Dion Fortune, The Mystical Qabalah, first published in London in 1935 (Weiser edition, 1999 printing). § numbers are Fortune’s paragraphs. “Row” is 777’s row: row 8 is Hod; Fortune’s chapter XXIII is Hod.

What it is

Hod means Splendour. (777, Cols. II and III, row 8.) Fortune translates it Glory. (Fortune, ch. XXIII, the table.) It is the eighth sphere.

Its heaven is Kokab, the Sphere of Mercury. (777, Cols. VI and VII, row 8.)

Fortune: it stands “at the foot of the Pillar of Severity”. (Fortune, ch. XXIII, the table.)

Fortune: Netzach “represents the instincts and the emotions they give rise to, and Hod represents the concrete mind.” (Fortune, ch. XXII, §1.)

Its virtue: truthfulness

777, in its column of Transcendental Morality, gives row 8 the word Truthfulness. (777, Col. L, row 8.)

Fortune: “how right it is that the Qabalah should give the supreme virtue as truthfulness”, in the sphere of “the concrete mind”. (Fortune, ch. XXIII, §37.)

Its vice: 777 gives Mercury (row 12) “Falsehood, Dishonesty [Envy]”; Fortune gives Falsehood, Dishonesty. (777, Col. L, row 12; Fortune, ch. XXIII, the table.)

Its spiritual experience: the Vision of Splendour

777: “The Vision of Splendour [Ezekiel]”. (777, Col. XLV, row 8.) Fortune gives the Vision of Splendour. (Fortune, ch. XXIII, the table.)

Fortune: it “is the realisation of the glory of God manifesting in the created world. The initiate of Hod sees behind the appearance of created things and discerns their Creator”. (Fortune, ch. XXIII, §36.)

Its God-name and the rest

  • God-name: Elohim Tzabaoth. 777’s note: with Jehovah Tzabaoth (Netzach) these “give respectively the two principal names of the Demiurge expressed in multiplicity and positive action. (Hosts.)” (777, Col. V, row 8, and its note.) Fortune: “the God of Hosts”. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Archangel: Michael (777 spells it Mikael). (777, Col. XCIX, row 8; Fortune, the table.)
  • Choir of angels: Beni Elohim; Fortune adds “Sons of God”. (777, Col. C, row 8; Fortune, the table.)
  • Magical image: “An Hermaphrodite.” (777, Col. CXXI, row 8; Fortune gives the same.)
  • Path of wisdom: the Absolute or Perfect Intelligence. (777, Col. XIII, row 8; Fortune, the table.)
  • Tarot: the four Eights. (777, Col. XIV, row 8; Fortune, the table.)
  • Colours, in 777’s four scales: King, violet purple; Queen, orange; Emperor (Yetzirah), red-russet; Empress, yellow-brown flecked white. (777, Cols. XV to XVIII, row 8.) Fortune gives violet-purple, orange, russet-red, and “yellowish black, flecked with white”. (Fortune, the table.)
  • In the body: the loins and legs. (Fortune, the table and §38.)

What it means for someone climbing

Fortune: “if we have no magical capacity, which is the work of the intellectual imagination, the Sphere of Hod will be a closed book to us.” (Fortune, ch. XXIII, §18.)

Fortune: “Life is the real initiator”; the grades are “designed to make conscious what was previously subconscious”. (Fortune, ch. XXIII, §18.)

Fortune: the keys to Yesod’s images “are to be found in Hod, the Sphere of Magic.” (Fortune, ch. XXIV, §14.) On this site Hod is the Library, behind the left door at Malkuth (path 31).

Left out
