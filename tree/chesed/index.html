Chesed · Mercy

The fourth sphere, in the centre of the Pillar of Mercy (Fortune), the first one up from Tiphareth on this site’s right. Every line below names its book. 777 comes first.

The books: 777 Revised, Aleister Crowley (the 1955 Neptune Press reprint of 777, 1909), in the Celephaïs Press text of 2004; and Dion Fortune, The Mystical Qabalah, first published in London in 1935 (Weiser edition, 1999 printing). § numbers are Fortune’s paragraphs. “Row” is 777’s row: row 4 is Chesed; Fortune’s chapter XVIII is Chesed.

What it is

Chesed means Mercy. It is the fourth sphere. (777, Cols. II and III, row 4.) Fortune gives it the further titles “Gedulah. Love. Majesty.” (Fortune, ch. XVIII, the table.)

Its heaven is Tzedeq, the Sphere of Jupiter. (777, Cols. VI and VII, row 4.)

Fortune: it stands “In the centre of the Pillar of Mercy.” (Fortune, ch. XVIII, the table.)

Fortune: “the first aspect of Microprosopos to come into being is Chesed, the Fourth Sephirah”, the first sphere below the Abyss. (Fortune, ch. XVIII, §§1 and 3.)

Fortune: “It is the loving father, the protector and preserver, just as Chokmah is the All-begetter.” (Fortune, ch. XVIII, §5.)

Its virtue: obedience

777, in its column of Transcendental Morality, gives row 4 the word Obedience. (777, Col. L, row 4.)

Fortune: “the virtue assigned to this sphere of experience is that of obedience. It is only through the virtue of obedience that the subject can profit by the wise rule of Chesed.” (Fortune, ch. XVIII, §22.)

Its vice: 777 gives Jupiter (row 21) “Bigotry, Hypocrisy [Gluttony]”; Fortune: “the vices assigned to Chesed—bigotry, hypocrisy, gluttony, and tyranny—are all social vices.” (777, Col. L, row 21; Fortune, ch. XVIII, §23.)

Its spiritual experience: the Vision of Love

777: “The Vision of Love”. (777, Col. XLV, row 4.) Fortune gives the same. (Fortune, ch. XVIII, the table.)

Its God-name and the rest

  • God-name: El. 777’s note: “The name AL is used in many senses.” (777, Col. V, row 4, and its note.) Fortune: El. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Archangel: Tzadkiel. (777, Col. XCIX, row 4; Fortune, the table.)
  • Choir of angels: Chashmalim, “Brilliant ones”. (777, Cols. C and CII, row 4.) Fortune spells it Chasmalim. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Magical image: “A mighty crowned and enthroned king”. (777, Col. CXXI, row 4.) Fortune: “A mighty crowned and throned king.” (Fortune, the table.)
  • Path of wisdom: the Measuring, Cohesive or Receptacular Intelligence. (777, Col. XIII, row 4.) Fortune: “the Cohesive or Receptive Intelligence”. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Tarot: the four Fours. (777, Col. XIV, row 4; Fortune, the table.)
  • Colours, in 777’s four scales: King, deep violet; Queen, blue; Emperor (Yetzirah), deep purple; Empress, deep azure flecked yellow. (777, Cols. XV to XVIII, row 4.) 777’s note: “The deep violet is episcopal.” (777, note to Col. XV, 4.) Fortune gives the same four by world. (Fortune, the table.)
  • In the body: the left arm. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Symbols: the solid figure, the tetrahedron, the pyramid, the equal-armed cross, the orb, the wand, the sceptre, the crook. (Fortune, the table.)

What it means for someone climbing

Fortune: “The occultist who does not possess the initiation of Chesed will be limited in his function to the sphere of Yesod, the plane of Maya, illusion.” With it, “He then becomes a master in the treasurehouse of images instead of being hallucinated by them.” (Fortune, ch. XVIII, §11.)

Fortune: “It is to the Sphere of Chesed that the exalted consciousness of the adept rises in his occult meditations; it is here that he receives the inspirations which he works out on the planes of form.” (Fortune, ch. XVIII, §19.)

On this site Chesed is the room of the Asked-for list, up from Tiphareth by path 20 and from Netzach by path 21; path 19 crosses to Geburah.
