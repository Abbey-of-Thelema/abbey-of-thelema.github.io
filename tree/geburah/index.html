Geburah · Strength

The fifth sphere, in the centre of the Pillar of Severity (Fortune), the first one up from Tiphareth on this site’s left. Every line below names its book. 777 comes first.

The books: 777 Revised, Aleister Crowley (the 1955 Neptune Press reprint of 777, 1909), in the Celephaïs Press text of 2004; and Dion Fortune, The Mystical Qabalah, first published in London in 1935 (Weiser edition, 1999 printing). § numbers are Fortune’s paragraphs. “Row” is 777’s row: row 5 is Geburah; Fortune’s chapter XIX is Geburah.

What it is

Geburah means Strength. It is the fifth sphere. (777, Cols. II and III, row 5.) Fortune: “Geburah, Strength, Severity”, with the further titles “Din: Justice. Pachad: Fear.” (Fortune, ch. XIX, the table.)

Its heaven is Madim, the Sphere of Mars. (777, Cols. VI and VII, row 5.)

Fortune: it stands “In the centre of the Pillar of Severity.” (Fortune, ch. XIX, the table.)

Fortune: “Binah is perpetually binding force into form, and Geburah perpetually breaking up and destroying all forms with its disruptive energy.” (Fortune, ch. XIX, §32.)

Fortune: “Geburah is the most dynamic and forceful of all the Sephiroth, but it is also the most highly disciplined.” (Fortune, ch. XIX, §47.)

Its virtue: energy

777, in its column of Transcendental Morality, gives row 5 the word Energy. (777, Col. L, row 5.)

Fortune gives “Energy. Courage.” (Fortune, ch. XIX, the table.) “The initiate of Geburah is a very dynamic and forceful person, but he is also a very controlled person. His characteristic virtues are an even temper and patience under provocation.” (Fortune, ch. XIX, §48.)

Its vice: 777 gives Mars (row 27) “Cruelty [Wrath]”; Fortune gives “Cruelty. Destruction.”, “his vices when these qualities run to excess”. (777, Col. L, row 27; Fortune, ch. XIX, the table and §40.)

Its spiritual experience: the Vision of Power

777: “The Vision of Power”. (777, Col. XLV, row 5.) Fortune: “The Spiritual Experience that is conveyed by initiation into the Sphere of Geburah is the Vision of Power.” (Fortune, ch. XIX, §38.)

Fortune: “The right handling of power is one of the greatest tests that can be imposed on any human being.” The one who has it “is the servant of the power he wields and must carry out its purposes, not serve his own.” (Fortune, ch. XIX, §38.)

Its God-name and the rest

  • God-name: Elohim Gibor. 777’s note: “The attribution is natural.” (777, Col. V, row 5, and its note.) Fortune spells it Elohim Gebor. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Archangel: Kamael. (777, Col. XCIX, row 5.) Fortune spells it Khamael. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Choir of angels: Seraphim, “Fiery serpents”. (777, Cols. C and CII, row 5; Fortune, the table.)
  • Magical image: “A mighty warrior in his chariot, armed and crowned”. (777, Col. CXXI, row 5.) Fortune: “A mighty warrior in his chariot.” (Fortune, the table.)
  • Path of wisdom: the Radical Intelligence. (777, Col. XIII, row 5; Fortune, the table.)
  • Tarot: the four Fives. (777, Col. XIV, row 5; Fortune, the table.)
  • Colours, in 777’s four scales: King, orange; Queen, scarlet red; Emperor (Yetzirah), bright scarlet; Empress, red flecked black. (777, Cols. XV to XVIII, row 5.) 777’s note: “The orange suggests the energy as opposed to other qualities of the Sun.” (777, note to Col. XV, 5.) Fortune gives the same four by world. (Fortune, the table.)
  • In the body: the right arm. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Symbols: the pentagon, the five-petalled Tudor rose, the sword, the spear, the scourge, the chain. (Fortune, the table.)

What it means for someone climbing

Fortune: “The discipline of Geburah must exactly equate with its energy”; “It is this tremendous Geburah discipline which is one of the testing-points of the Mysteries.” (Fortune, ch. XIX, §47.)

Fortune: “The initiate of Mars is essentially the Happy Warrior, the initiate who has passed through the grade of Tiphareth and gained equilibrium.” (Fortune, ch. XIX, §48.)

On this site Geburah is the room of the Debate Hall and Death’s Record, up from Tiphareth by path 22 and from Hod by path 23; path 19 crosses to Chesed.

Left out
