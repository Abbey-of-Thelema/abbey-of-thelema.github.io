Daath · Knowledge

Not one of the ten. Fortune: the “invisible Sephirah” that is “situated astride the Abyss”. On this site it has no room; the Abyss page stands where she puts it. Every line below names its book. 777 comes first.

The books: 777 Revised, Aleister Crowley (the 1955 Neptune Press reprint of 777, 1909), in the Celephaïs Press text of 2004; and Dion Fortune, The Mystical Qabalah, first published in London in 1935 (Weiser edition, 1999 printing). § numbers are Fortune’s paragraphs. “Row” is 777’s row: 777 gives Daath no row of its own; Fortune’s chapter VII is the Three Supernals and chapter XI the Subjective Sephiroth.

What it is

Daath means Knowledge. (Fortune, ch. VII, §3.) 777 gives it no row in its tables, which run from 0 to 32 with 31 bis and 32 bis; it names it only in its notes. (777, Table I, the row numbers.)

777, on Tiphareth’s God-name: “The reference is to Tiphereth as the child of Chokmah and Binah, Daäth (their first child) having failed to find a place on the Tree.” (777, note to Col. V, 6.)

Fortune: “pendent to these two latter Sephiroth is a curious and mysterious third, which is never represented in the glyph of the Tree; this is the invisible Sephirah, Daath, Knowledge, and it is said to be formed out of the conjunction of Chokmah and Binah and is situated astride the Abyss.” (Fortune, ch. VII, §3.)

Fortune: “It is in the Abyss that Daath, the Invisible Sephirah, has its station, and it might aptly be named the Sephirah of Becoming.” (Fortune, ch. XVIII, §2.)

No virtue, vice, God-name or choir is given

Fortune: Daath is the sphere “to which no Deity-name or angelic host is assigned and which has no mundane symbol in planet or element, as have all the other stations on the Tree.” (Fortune, ch. VII, §15.) 777’s column of Transcendental Morality has no Daath line either. (777, Col. L.)

What it holds

Fortune: “in Daath is the secret of both generation and regeneration, the key to the manifestation of all things through the differentiation into pairs of Opposites and their union in a Third.” (Fortune, ch. VII, §19.)

Fortune: “Daath is usually held to represent the consciousness of another dimension, or the consciousness of another level or plane; it essentially represents the idea of change of key.” (Fortune, ch. XI, §11.)

What 777 does give it

  • A planet: “Uranus is attributed to Daäth because of its explosive nature. The Abyss is represented in Nature by the Asteroids.” (777, explanation of Col. VI, 2.)
  • Saturn in the hexagram: “Saturn is attributed to Daäth in the hexagram of the planets. This is the creative Saturn, the hidden God”. (777, explanation of Col. VI, 3.)
  • Colours, in the four scales: “Daath—Lavender, Grey-white, Pure violet, Grey flecked gold.” (777, notes to Cols. XV to XVIII, p. 43.)
  • In the body: “Cerebrum medium, cuius locus est in parte capitis postica” (777, notes, Col. CIII.) Fortune: “the nape of the neck, the point where the spine meets the skull”. (Fortune, ch. XI, §11.)

What it means for someone climbing

Fortune: “Daath is situated at the point where the Abyss bisects the Middle Pillar, and that up the Middle Pillar lies the Path of the Arrow, the way by which consciousness goes when the psychic rises on the planes”. (Fortune, ch. VII, §19.)

On this site Daath has no room and no door. The Abyss page, past Tiphareth, is where it would stand.

Left out

777 gives Daath no row, so it has no grade to leave off (777, Table I). Lines that begin “On this site”, and the words “this site’s” in the opening line, describe this site’s own doors, not a book. Nothing else on this page comes from anywhere but the two books named above.
