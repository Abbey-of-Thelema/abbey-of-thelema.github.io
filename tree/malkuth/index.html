Malkuth · The Kingdom

The tenth sphere and the ground floor of the Tree, where the climb starts. Every line below names its book. 777 comes first.

The books: 777 Revised, Aleister Crowley (the 1955 Neptune Press reprint of 777, 1909), in the Celephaïs Press text of 2004; and Dion Fortune, The Mystical Qabalah, first published in London in 1935 (Weiser edition, 1999 printing), chapter XXV, “Malkuth”. § numbers are Fortune’s paragraphs.

What it is

Malkuth means the Kingdom. It is the tenth sphere. (777, Cols. II and III, row 10.)

Its heaven is Cholem Yesodoth, the Sphere of the Elements. (777, Cols. VI and VII, row 10.) Fortune: it is the one sphere divided into four quarters, for Earth, Air, Fire and Water. (Fortune, §1.)

It sits at the base of the middle pillar, the Pillar of Equilibrium. It is in none of the Tree’s three triangles, and “it receives the influences or emanations of all the other Sephiroth”. (Fortune, the table and §1.)

“The great point to remember in connection with Malkuth is that herein is achieved stability.” (Fortune, §5.)

Its virtue: scepticism and discrimination

777, in its column of Transcendental Morality, gives row 10 the word Scepticism. (777, Col. L, row 10.)

Fortune names Malkuth’s virtue as Discrimination. (Fortune, the table and §90.) She explains it as a filter: Malkuth casts out what is spent and keeps “that which still retains its usefulness”. (Fortune, §91.)

“It is discrimination which enables us to know which is the lesser value that has to be given up in order to obtain the greater.” (Fortune, §93.)

Its vices, in Fortune: avarice and inertia. Stability overdone becomes sluggishness; avarice is holding on to what should be let go. (Fortune, the table and §92.)

Its spiritual experience: the Vision of the Holy Guardian Angel

777: “The Vision of the Holy Guardian Angel or of Adonai.” (777, Col. XLV, row 10.) Fortune gives the same. (Fortune, the table and §87.)

777’s note on the God-names says Adonai “is one title of the Holy Guardian Angel”. (777, notes to Col. V.)

Fortune calls this vision “the bringing down of the Godhead into manhood, just as the spiritual experience of Tiphareth is the taking up of manhood into the Godhead.” (Fortune, §89.)

Its God-name and the rest

  • God-name: Adonai Melekh, “My Lord the King”, who “is the natural inhabitant of ‘The Kingdom.’” (777, Col. V, row 10, and its note.) Fortune spells it Adonai Malekh, and adds Adonai ha Aretz. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Archangel: Sandalphon. (777, Col. XCIX, row 10.)
  • Choir of angels: Ashim, “Souls of Fire”. (777, Col. C, row 10; Fortune, the table.)
  • Magical image: “A young woman crowned and veiled.” (777, Col. CXXI, row 10.) Fortune has “crowned and throned”. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Path of wisdom: the Resplendent Intelligence. (777, Col. XIII, row 10; Fortune quotes the whole Yetziratic text.)
  • Tarot: the four Tens. (777, Col. XIV, row 10; Fortune, the table.)
  • Colours, in 777’s four scales: King scale, yellow; Queen scale, “citrine, olive, russet, and black”; Emperor scale (Yetzirah), “as Queen scale, but flecked with gold”; Empress scale, “black rayed yellow”. (777, Cols. XV, XVI, XVII and XVIII, row 10.) Fortune gives the same four, by world: Atziluth yellow; Briah citrine, olive, russet and black; Yetzirah the same, “flecked with gold”; Assiah “black, rayed with yellow”. (Fortune, the table.)
  • In the body: the feet. (Fortune, the table and §94.)

What it means for someone climbing

“One of the most important things we have to do before we can rise out of the limitations of life in Malkuth and breathe a wider air, is to learn how to let go.” That is discrimination at work. (Fortune, §93.)

Malkuth has one neighbour, Yesod, and “no understanding of Malkuth can be arrived at save through an understanding of Yesod.” (Fortune, §6.) On this site the centre door, path 32, goes up to Yesod.

Keep your feet on the ground: “unless the feet are firmly planted on Mother Earth, no stability is possible.” (Fortune, §94.)
