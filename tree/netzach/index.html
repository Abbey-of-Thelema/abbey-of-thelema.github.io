Netzach · Victory

The seventh sphere, at the foot of the Pillar of Mercy (Fortune), behind this site’s right door at Malkuth. Every line below names its book. 777 comes first.

The books: 777 Revised, Aleister Crowley (the 1955 Neptune Press reprint of 777, 1909), in the Celephaïs Press text of 2004; and Dion Fortune, The Mystical Qabalah, first published in London in 1935 (Weiser edition, 1999 printing). § numbers are Fortune’s paragraphs. “Row” is 777’s row: row 7 is Netzach; Fortune’s chapter XXII is Netzach.

What it is

Netzach means Victory. It is the seventh sphere. (777, Cols. II and III, row 7.)

Its heaven is Nogah, the Sphere of Venus. (777, Cols. VI and VII, row 7.)

Fortune: it stands “at the foot of the Pillar of Mercy”. (Fortune, ch. XXII, the table.)

Fortune: Netzach and Hod represent “force and form on a lower arc”; “Netzach represents the instincts and the emotions they give rise to”. (Fortune, ch. XXII, §1.)

Its virtue: unselfishness

777, in its column of Transcendental Morality, gives row 7 the word Unselfishness. (777, Col. L, row 7.)

Fortune: “The virtue is Unselfishness—that is to say, the capacity to polarise from the negative pole.” (Fortune, ch. XXII, §33.)

Its vice: 777 gives Venus (row 14) “Unchastity [Lust]”; Fortune: “the obvious ones of love abused—unchastity and lust.” (777, Col. L, row 14; Fortune, ch. XXII, §33.)

Its spiritual experience: the Vision of Beauty Triumphant

777: “The Vision of Beauty Triumphant.” (777, Col. XLV, row 7.) Fortune gives the same. (Fortune, ch. XXII, the table and §33.)

Its God-name and the rest

  • God-name: Jehovah Tzabaoth. 777’s note: with Elohim Tzabaoth (Hod) these “give respectively the two principal names of the Demiurge expressed in multiplicity and positive action. (Hosts.)” (777, Col. V, row 7, and its note.) Fortune: “The Lord of Hosts”. (Fortune, the table.)
  • Archangel: Haniel. (777, Col. XCIX, row 7.)
  • Choir of angels: Elohim; Fortune adds “Gods”. (777, Col. C, row 7; Fortune, the table.)
  • Magical image: “A beautiful naked woman.” (777, Col. CXXI, row 7; Fortune gives the same.)
  • Path of wisdom: the Occult or Hidden Intelligence. (777, Col. XIII, row 7; Fortune, the table.)
  • Tarot: the four Sevens. (777, Col. XIV, row 7; Fortune, the table.)
  • Colours, in 777’s four scales: King, amber; Queen, emerald; Emperor (Yetzirah), bright yellow green; Empress, olive flecked gold. (777, Cols. XV to XVIII, row 7.) Fortune gives nearly the same four by world: amber, emerald, “Bright yellowish green” and “Olive, flecked with gold”. (Fortune, the table.)
  • In the body: the loins, hips and legs. (Fortune, the table and §34.)
  • Symbols: the lamp, the girdle and the rose. (Fortune, the table and §35.)

What it means for someone climbing

Fortune: “If there is no Venus in our own nature, no capacity to respond to the call of love, the gates of the Sphere of Netzach will never open to us”. (Fortune, ch. XXIII, §18.)

Fortune: “It is only when the aspirant, having the capacity to respond on all planes to the call of Venus, can easily and without effort refrain at will from responding, that he can be made an initiate of the Sphere of Netzach. This is why it is said of the adept that he has the use of all things, but is dependent upon nothing.” (Fortune, ch. XXIII, §19.)

On this site Netzach is the Antichrist room, behind the right door at Malkuth (path 29).
