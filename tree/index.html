The Tree of Life

Ten stations joined by twenty-two paths. This page is a side room: the decodes use it as a map, and this is the legend.

Each path carries a Hebrew letter, a planet, element or sign, and a tarot card. Tap a number or a name on the drawing to jump to its explanation below. Gold lines are the three doors off the ground floor.

Cards follow the house’s table: the Star on path 15 (Heh) and the Emperor on path 28 (Tzaddi), as Crowley set them on the word of Liber AL I:57. The printed rows 15 and 28 of 777 show the older order, the Emperor on 15 and the Star on 28, and those two entries say so. The editorial note in 777 Revised (“The Atus of Thoth”) says to exchange lines 15 and 28 on every column built on the Zodiac or the Tarot, so the powers column goes with the cards: Astrology on 15, the Power of Consecrating Things on 28. Where the Thoth name of a card differs, it is given beside it. The Waite lines are his upright meanings from The Pictorial Key to the Tarot (1911, pp. 283–287), word for word; … marks where his list goes on.

The ten stations

1 · Kether

The Crown

Sphere
the Primum Mobile
Pillar
Middle Pillar
God-name
Eheieh
Archangel
Metatron
Vision
Union with God (777)

Kether is the first point, the top of the Tree, before anything has been divided into two. 777 gives it the plain title Crown and calls its attainment Union with God. Everything below it is the same point seen from further and further away, which is why it has no path above it. In a song it almost never shows up as a word. It shows up as a feeling of one thing under everything: a single voice, a single note, a line that says nobody and nothing is separate. When a decode reaches Kether, the claim is that the song ends where it began and that the beginning was also the goal.

2 · Chokmah

Wisdom

Sphere
the sphere of the fixed stars (the Zodiac)
Pillar
Pillar of Mercy
God-name
Jah (Yah)
Archangel
Ratziel
Vision
The Vision of God face to face (777)

Chokmah is the first motion outward from Kether, the first division of one into two. 777 lists its virtue as Devotion and its vision as God face to face. It is the active, fatherly side of the top triangle: pure force with no form yet. In song-reading it is the spark or the wish before it has a shape: the first "I want", the push to begin the climb. It sits on the Pillar of Mercy, the right-hand column, and it joins the Crown by path 11, the Fool.

3 · Binah

Understanding

Sphere
Saturn
Pillar
Pillar of Severity
God-name
YHVH Elohim
Archangel
Tzaphkiel
Vision
The Vision of Sorrow (777)

Binah is the Great Mother: the sphere that gives Chokmah's force a form, and with form comes time and limit. 777 gives it Saturn, Silence as its virtue, and the Vision of Sorrow. It is the left-hand partner of Chokmah on the Pillar of Severity. Songs touch Binah when they sing about time, age, grief that has been made into a shape, mothers, the sea, the dark that holds things. Its door down the Tree is path 17, the Lovers, to Tiphareth.

4 · Chesed

Mercy

Sphere
Jupiter
Pillar
Pillar of Mercy
God-name
El
Archangel
Tzadkiel
Vision
The Vision of Love (777)

Chesed is the builder and the giver, the first sphere below the Abyss. 777 gives it Jupiter, Obedience as its virtue, and the Vision of Love. It is the merciful king: law as generosity, order that holds people up. In a song it appears as abundance, a benevolent father or ruler, a kingdom that works. 777's table gives Jupiter's sin as bigotry and hypocrisy, which reads as mercy turned into a rule you impose on others. It faces Geburah across the Tree, and the two are read together as give and cut.

5 · Geburah

Strength

Sphere
Mars
Pillar
Pillar of Severity
God-name
Elohim Gibor
Archangel
Kamael
Vision
The Vision of Power (777)

Geburah is severity: the force that cuts away what Chesed has allowed to grow too large. 777 gives it Mars, Energy as the virtue, and the Vision of Power. It is the sword, the judge, the fire that clears. In a decode it marks where something has to be burnt, ended or cut for the climb to continue. The house always states that the thing that dies at Geburah is a way of living, never a person. It is paired with Chesed and both lead into Tiphareth, through path 22 (Justice) and path 20 (the Hermit).

6 · Tiphareth

Beauty

Sphere
the Sun
Pillar
Middle Pillar
God-name
YHVH Eloah ve-Daath
Archangel
Raphael
Vision
The Vision of the Harmony of Things, also the Mysteries of the Crucifixion (777)

Tiphareth is the heart and centre of the Tree, the sphere of the Sun. Eight paths meet in it (13, 15, 17, 20, 22, 24, 25 and 26), more than in any other sphere. 777 gives its virtue as Devotion to the Great Work and its vision as the Harmony of Things, also the Mysteries of the Crucifixion. It is where the dying self and the risen self are the same figure: the child, the sacrificed god and the king in one. Many of the house's decodes end here: the song's real answer, the moment the singer is no longer split. Kether is rarely reached, so Tiphareth is usually the top of the climb.

7 · Netzach

Victory

Sphere
Venus
Pillar
Pillar of Mercy
God-name
YHVH Tzabaoth
Archangel
Haniel
Vision
The Vision of Beauty Triumphant (777)

Netzach is desire and feeling: the sphere of Venus, on the Pillar of Mercy below Chesed. 777 gives its virtue as Unselfishness and its vision as Beauty Triumphant. In song it is where the singer wants, aches, burns or loves with no argument attached. It is the heart's side of the house's reading, as Hod is the mind's. 777's table gives Venus's sin as unchastity (lust), which reads as desire that grabs instead of giving. It is reached from Tiphareth by path 24 (Death), from Chesed by path 21, and it opens onto Yesod by path 28 and Malkuth by path 29.

8 · Hod

Splendour

Sphere
Mercury
Pillar
Pillar of Severity
God-name
Elohim Tzabaoth
Archangel
Michael
Vision
The Vision of Splendour (777)

Hod is mind and language: the sphere of Mercury, on the Pillar of Severity opposite Netzach. 777 gives its virtue as Truthfulness and its vision as Splendour. It is where the singer explains, argues, names things and builds the word-structure of the song. A decode reads rap verses, fast lyrics and anything about words or numbers at Hod. 777's table gives Mercury's sin as falsehood and dishonesty, which reads as telling yourself the story that avoids the truth. It connects down to Yesod by path 30 and to Malkuth by path 31, and up to Geburah and Tiphareth.

9 · Yesod

The Foundation

Sphere
the Moon
Pillar
Middle Pillar
God-name
Shaddai El Chai
Archangel
Gabriel
Vision
The Vision of the Machinery of the Universe (777)

Yesod is the foundation, the sphere of the Moon, and the mirror under the day. 777 gives its virtue as Independence and its vision as the Machinery of the Universe. Dreams, memory, reflection, the unconscious and the storehouse of images all sit here. In song-reading it is where a singer is looking at themselves, replaying the past, or caught between two selves. It is the last sphere before the ground. Path 25, Temperance (Sagittarius), joins it to Tiphareth: the arrow shot straight up the middle.

10 · Malkuth

The Kingdom

Sphere
the elements (Earth)
Pillar
Middle Pillar
God-name
Adonai Melekh
Archangel
Sandalphon
Vision
The Vision of the Holy Guardian Angel or of Adonai (777)

Malkuth is the ground floor: the world as it looks and the body that lives in it. 777 gives it the elements, Adonai Melekh as God-name, and the Vision of the Holy Guardian Angel. In the house method nearly every song starts here, at the floor: stuck, numb, cold, dark or asleep. Three paths lead off the floor: path 32 (Tau, the World), path 31 (Shin, Judgement) and path 29 (Qoph, the Moon). The house calls these the three doors, and finding which one a song takes is the first step of every decode. The song may also end here again, changed: the last chorus is the first chorus with someone now standing in it.

The twenty-two paths

Path 11 · Aleph

The Fool

Joins
Kether and Chokmah
Letter
Aleph, “ox”
Rules
Air
Card
The Fool
Waite
“Folly, mania, extravagance, intoxication, delirium, frenzy, bewrayment.”
777 power
Divination

The first path, between the Crown and Wisdom, is the Fool: zero, the empty hand before the first move. Aleph is the breath, the wind, the step off the cliff with the sun behind you. Songs touch it with leaps, wandering, new starts, wind in the hair and trust without a plan. It is rarely used as a gate, because it sits far above the floor, but a decode may name it when a line carries pure beginning.

Path 12 · Beth

The Magician

Joins
Kether and Binah
Letter
Beth, “house”
Rules
Mercury
Card
The Magician (Thoth: The Magus)
Waite
“Skill, diplomacy, address, subtlety …”
777 power
Miracles of healing, gift of tongues, knowledge of sciences

Beth is the house, and the Magician is the one who builds it with will and tools: the four suits laid on a table, one hand up and one hand down. This path joins the Crown and Understanding and carries Mercury, the planet of speech. In a song it is any line about words made into action, skill, a trick that works, making something from what is on the table.

Path 13 · Gimel

The High Priestess

Gimel, “camel”

Card
The High Priestess (Thoth: The Priestess)
Waite
“Secrets, mystery, the future as yet unrevealed …”
777 power
The White Tincture, clairvoyance, divination by dreams

This is the long middle road from Tiphareth to the Crown, the longest on the Tree, and it crosses the Abyss. The High Priestess sits between the white and black pillars with the veil behind her, guarding what is not yet revealed. Gimel the camel is the crosser of deserts. Songs touch it when a singer faces a mystery, a silence, a veil, a moon that keeps a secret, or a trip too long to see its end.

Path 14 · Daleth

Joins

Daleth, “door”
Rules
Venus

The Empress

Daleth is the door, and the Empress is the one who stands in it: fertility, abundance, the garden. The path joins Wisdom and Understanding, the father and mother of the Tree, so it is the marriage of the two supernals. Venus rules it. In a song it is love as creative power, pregnancy and birth images, flowers, a lush world, a love that makes something rather than only wants.

Path 15 · Heh

The Star

Joins
Chokmah and Tiphareth
Letter
Heh, “window”
Rules
Aquarius
Card
The Star
Waite
“Loss, theft, privation, abandonment; another reading says—hope and bright prospects.”
777 power
Astrology

Heh is the window, the opening in the wall. The house reads this path with the Star and Aquarius: a naked figure pouring water on land and sea under eight-pointed stars, hope seen through the window. Older table order: 777's printed row 15 and the Golden Dawn put the Emperor and Aries here; Crowley exchanged the Star and the Emperor on the word of Liber AL I:57 (“Tzaddi is not the Star”), and 777 Revised's editorial note records the exchange. The note exchanges lines 15 and 28 on every column built on the Zodiac or the Tarot, and the column of magical powers (XLV) is in its list. 777 prints Power of Consecrating Things on row 15 and Astrology on row 28; with the exchange, row 15 reads Astrology. In a song it is hope after the fall, wishes, the night sky and water poured out.

Table note: Lines 15 and 28 exchanged on the tarot, zodiac and powers columns, as the editorial note in 777 Revised directs: 777 Revised, “Editorial Note: The Atus of Thoth” (printed after Table I, before “Various Arrangements”); Liber AL I:57.

Vau is the nail, the thing that fastens one thing to another. The Hierophant is the teacher at the altar between two pillars, joining heaven and earth through ritual and tradition. Taurus, the bull, belongs to this path, and 777 lists the secret of physical strength. Songs touch it with church, vows, teachers, rules handed down, bells, and the sense of being bound to something old on purpose.

Zain is the sword that divides, and the Lovers is the card of choice: two figures under an angel, a decision between two things. Gemini, the twins, owns this path, and it links Understanding to Tiphareth. In song-reading it appears as the two voices, the two selves, a fork in the road, the lover who is also the other half of the singer. The house often notes when a song with two singers is one person talking to themselves.

Cheth is the fence and the Chariot is the card of the driver carried in a shell: a charioteer with no reins, held in by a canopy of stars. Cancer, the crab, the shell and the home, rules it, joining Understanding and Severity. Songs touch it with driving, journeys in a protected shell, armour, a self held together by a hard outside, and motion that is controlled by the will instead of by force.

Path 19 · Teth

Strength

Joins
Chesed and Geburah
Letter
Teth, “serpent”
Rules
Leo
Card
Strength (Thoth: Lust)
Waite
“Power, energy, action, courage, magnanimity …”

Power of training wild beasts

Teth is the serpent. The card is Strength on Waite's card and Lust in Crowley's Thoth: a woman closing the jaws of a lion without effort. Leo, the lion, is its sign, and it connects Mercy and Severity, the two sides of the Tree. In song it is love and courage as taming rather than killing: facing the beast in yourself, passion made friendly, calm authority. A decode that reads it takes the lion as a part of the singer.

Path 20 · Yod

The Hermit

Joins
Chesed and Tiphareth
Letter
Yod, “hand”
Rules
Virgo
Card
The Hermit
Waite
“Prudence, circumspection …”
777 power
Invisibility, parthenogenesis, initiation (?)

Yod is the hand, the smallest letter, the first of the Name. The Hermit stands on a snowy peak with a lantern and a staff, light in one hand and support in the other. Virgo is the sign. The path joins Mercy to Tiphareth. In songs it is solitude on purpose: a long walk alone, a light carried in the dark, an old teacher, the one who goes up the hill first so others can follow the light.

Path 21 · Kaph

Wheel of Fortune

Joins
Chesed and Netzach
Letter
Kaph, “palm of the hand”
Rules
Jupiter
Card
Wheel of Fortune (Thoth: Fortune)
Waite

Power of acquiring political and other ascendancy

Kaph is the palm of the hand that holds or lets go. The Wheel of Fortune turns with a sphinx on top and figures rising and falling around it. Jupiter, the planet of expansion, rules it, and the path runs from Mercy down to Victory. Songs show it as luck, chance, fortune and bad fortune, cycles, years going round, and the feeling that the same things keep coming back. The song's turning point is often a wheel when the singer stops fighting the cycle.

Path 22 · Lamed

Justice

Joins

Lamed, “ox-goad”
Rules
Libra

Justice (Thoth: Adjustment)
Waite
“Equity, rightness, probity, executive …”

Lamed is the ox-goad, the tool that moves you in a straight line. The card is Justice on Waite's card and in 777, and Adjustment in Crowley's Thoth: scales and a sword in perfect balance. Libra owns it. It joins Severity to Tiphareth. In a song it is consequences: the debt, the balance sheet, the verdict, getting what you gave. Decodes read it as the moment the singer accepts the truth of what they did without hiding.

Joins

“Wisdom, circumspection, discernment, trials, sacrifice, intuition, divination, prophecy.”

Mem is water, and the Hanged Man is the figure hung by one foot with a calm face and a halo. The path joins Severity to Splendour, and in the house's reading it is the ego death: giving up the position you were holding so you can see the world upside down and be changed. Waite's meaning is not harm, and the house always says so. Songs touch it with drowning, sacrifice, surrender, being suspended, baptism or waiting under water.

Path 24 · Nun

Death

Joins
Tiphareth and Netzach
Letter
Nun, “fish”
Rules
Scorpio
Card
Death
Waite
“End, mortality, destruction, corruption; also, for a man, the loss of a benefactor; for a woman, many contrarieties; for a maid, failure of marriage projects.”

Necromancy

Nun is the fish, and Death is the card, with Scorpio as the sign. It joins Tiphareth to Victory. Death here is transformation, the one thing that has to die for the next thing to live: a skeleton, a rose banner, a river and a sun rising. In a song it appears as endings of an old self, scorpions, venom, funerals, rebirth by water. Decodes that use it state clearly that what dies is the self, not the body.

Path 25 · Samekh

Samekh is the prop or tent-peg, and the card is Temperance on Waite's card (777 spells it Temperence) and Art in Crowley's Thoth: an angel pouring water between two cups, one foot on land and one on water. Sagittarius, the archer, is its sign, and the path runs straight up the Middle Pillar from the Moon to the Sun, the arrow shot upward. In song it is the middle of the climb: the mixing of opposites, patience, the long straight road, the arrow aimed at a single mark.

Ayin is the eye, and the Devil is the card, with Capricorn the goat as the sign. It runs from Tiphareth to Splendour. Waite shows a horned figure above two chained people whose chains are loose enough to lift off. In songs it is temptation, bondage that is chosen, obsession, the ego's grip, a laugh in the dark. The reading is that the chains were never locked, and the singer has to see it to leave.
