Taylor Swift Met Herself on the Shore — The Hidden Map in Out of the Woods, Decode #33

Watch the end of Out of the Woods again. Taylor comes out of the woods onto the beach, and another version of her is already standing there. The wolves, the twenty stitches, the necklace, the sunrise: every line walks her back to that shore.

Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.

Thelema is Greek for Will. It is the fixed direction a person keeps, not the wish that changes by the hour.

THE COUCH

“the rest of the world was black and white… we were in screaming color.”

PROFANE

A couple and a picture that makes everything else look dull.

INITIATE

The Ten of Cups shows a man and a woman under a rainbow. Arthur Edward Waite wrote the guide to the deck; Pamela Colman Smith painted the cards. Tens sit at Malkuth, the ordinary room at the bottom of the Tree of Life.

Thomas 84 says when you see your likeness, you rejoice. 777 calls the Moon’s path the Magic Mirror that reflects astral forms. The album cover is a Polaroid.

The Polaroid that makes the rest of the world gray.

THE QUESTION

“Are we out of the woods yet?”

PROFANE

The question after the scare.

INITIATE

Waite writes that the path between the towers is the issue into the unknown. 777 gives this path the power Bewitchments, Casting Illusions. Its letter note reads Illusionary is the Initiator of Disorder.

The video places wolves in those woods. 777 describes the wanderer on a path beset by the hell-hounds, then through the balanced Elements to the Dawn. Qoph is the Hebrew letter of the Moon card. It means back of the head. The song is built on looking at it now and on remembering.

Wolves at the gate.

The issue into the unknown.

THE SEASON

“built to fall apart / Then fall back together.”

PROFANE

A fragile season that breaks and mends.

INITIATE

Liber AL I:29 says “For I am divided for love’s sake, for the chance of union.” I:30 says the pain of division is as nothing, and the joy of dissolution all.

The Gospel of Thomas, saying 11, says on the day when you were one, you became divided. The Wheel of Fortune is path 21, the letter Kaph. 777 gives 20 as its number. Chance is sung later in the same verse. Crowley calls the Moon card midnight and the last stage of winter. The song sets its memory in December, at night.

THE NECKLACE

“Your necklace hanging from my neck.”

PROFANE

Something left behind.

INITIATE

The Devil is path 26. Its path leaves Hod. Waite shows two figures, a man and a woman, chained at the neck. The necklace is already on her.

THE AIRPLANES

“Two paper airplanes flying.”

PROFANE

Something they made and let go.

INITIATE

The Eight of Wands sits at Hod. Waite describes a flight of wands through open country, at hand, on the threshold. 777 titles the card Swiftness. Row 25’s weapon is the Arrow sped through the air.

THE CRASH

“Twenty stitches in a hospital room.”

PROFANE

The accident.

INITIATE

The Tower is path 27, the letter Peh. Waite’s meaning includes unforeseen catastrophe. The hidden message printed in the album booklet for this track is “They loved each other recklessly.” Twenty is the number of the letter Kaph, the Wheel of Fortune. The stitches call back to the turn that left Netzach.

Unforeseen catastrophe.

THE MIRROR

“When you started crying, baby I did too.”

PROFANE

Both of them in the hospital.

INITIATE

Psalm 30:2 says “I cried unto thee, and thou hast healed me.” Verse 5 says “weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning.” The bridge has the cry, the healing, then the sunrise. The stitches are the healing. The pronouns have not yet traded.

THE TREES

“the monsters turned out to be just trees.”

PROFANE

The thing she feared wasn’t real.

INITIATE

Mark 8:24-25. The half-healed man saw men as trees, walking, then saw every man clearly. The song runs the order the other way. Thomas 5 says recognize what is in your sight and the hidden becomes plain.

Liber AL II:9 says the sorrows are but as shadows; they pass and are done; but there is that which remains. 777’s note for the Moon’s letter: Illusionary is the Initiator of Disorder.

The light that clears the woods.

THE TRADE

“When the sun came up you were looking at me.”

PROFANE

Morning.

INITIATE

1 Corinthians 13:12 says now we see through a glass, darkly; but then face to face.

Qoph is Back of head. Resh is Head. 777’s note for Resh: the Twins shining forth and playing. In the Sun is the Secret of the Spirit. Crowley writes there is a budding morrow in midnight. The gate card already held this sunrise.

THE RETURN

In the video she comes out of the woods onto the beach. Another version of her is already standing there.

PROFANE

She ends where she started.

INITIATE

The vision of sphere 10 is the Vision of the Holy Guardian Angel or of Adonai. The last chorus is the first chorus with somebody standing in it. 777 says the Moon path is the essence of error and the Great Work is not accomplished. The song starts on that path and finishes on the shore.

The earlier self waiting on the shore she started from.

The walk and the return to the same ground.

She sees the trees for what they are in the bridge. Why is the last thing she sings still the question?
