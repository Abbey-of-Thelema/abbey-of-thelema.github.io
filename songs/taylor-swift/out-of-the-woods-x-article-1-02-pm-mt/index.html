THE DECODE, AS POSTED

🔮 .:Out of the Woods — Taylor Swift:. .:ANTICHRIST DECODE #33:.

Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.

Thelema is Greek for Will. It is the fixed direction a person keeps, not the wish that changes by the hour.

THREE THINGS THAT NEED NO TRAINING

Mark 8:18 asks “do ye not remember?” Verse 24 has the half-healed man say “I see men as trees, walking.” Verse 25 says he was restored and “saw every man clearly.” The scene is a blind man healed in two touches. Mark has “remember,” “trees” and “clearly.” The song has “remember,” “trees” and “in the clear.”

Psalm 30:2 says “I cried unto thee, and thou hast healed me.” Verse 5 says “weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning.” The bridge has the crying, the stitches, then the sun comes up.

In the video wolves chase her through the woods. Waite wrote that the dog and the wolf are the fears of the natural mind in the presence of that place of exit.

Wolves at the gate. Waite’s own words on the Moon.

THE FLOOR

Malkuth

The Kingdom. The ordinary room. Tens sit at Malkuth.

“the rest of the world was black and white… we were in screaming color.”

PROFANE

A couple and a picture that makes everything else look dull.

INITIATE

The Ten of Cups shows a man and a woman under a rainbow. Arthur Edward Waite wrote the guide to the Rider-Waite deck; Pamela Colman Smith painted it. His guide describes the card as cups in a rainbow, with a man and a woman below it looking up in wonder. The Polaroid is the colored frame. The rest of the world is the black-and-white outside it. The album cover is itself a Polaroid. The lyric lives inside the medium of the record.

The Polaroid that makes the rest of the world gray.

THE GATE

Path 29, Qoph, the Moon

Woods. Night fear. The way out. Qoph is the Hebrew letter of the Moon card.

“Are we out of the woods yet?”

PROFANE

The question after the scare.

INITIATE

Waite’s Pictorial Key to the Tarot says the path between the towers is the issue into the unknown. The video places wolves in those woods. Pisces is the sign on the Moon card. Crowley, in The Book of Thoth, calls Pisces “the last stage of winter” and says “this card represents midnight.” The song sets its memory in December, at night. 777, Crowley’s book of correspondences, gives Qoph as the back of the head. The song is built on looking at it now and on remembering.

The issue into the unknown.

VERSE 2

Netzach

Victory. Desire.

“built to fall apart / Then fall back together.”

PROFANE

A fragile season that breaks and mends.

INITIATE

The Wheel of Fortune is path 21, the letter Kaph. The Wheel is the road that leaves Netzach. Chance and the turn sit on that road. The Gospel of Thomas, a collection of sayings found in Egypt in 1945, has saying 11: “On the day when you were one, you became divided.” The line holds both the break and the return.

“your necklace hanging from my neck.”

PROFANE

Something left behind.

INITIATE

These are Hod’s pictures, shown in verse 2 before the crash carries the song there. The Devil is path 26. Its path leaves Hod. Waite shows two figures, a man and a woman, chained at the neck. The necklace on the neck is that picture.

“Two paper airplanes flying.”

PROFANE

Something they made and let go.

INITIATE

The Eight of Wands sits at Hod. Waite describes a flight of wands through open country, at hand, on the threshold. The chance they stood is still in the air.

THE CRASH

Path 27, Peh, the Tower

Peh is the letter of the Tower card.

“Twenty stitches in a hospital room.”

PROFANE

The accident.

INITIATE

The path lands in Hod. Waite’s meaning for the Tower includes “unforeseen catastrophe.” The hidden message printed in the album booklet for this track is “They loved each other recklessly.” The bridge is a crash. 777 gives 20 as the number of the letter Kaph, the Wheel of Fortune. The stitches call back to the Wheel that left Netzach. Chance is in the hospital room.

Unforeseen catastrophe.

THE CRYING

Hod

Splendor. The mind that sorts what happened. Path 27 lands here.

“you started crying, baby, I did too.”

PROFANE

Both of them in the hospital.

INITIATE

This is Psalm 30 from the top of the page, in order: the cry, the healing, the morning. The stitches are the healing.

THE LIGHT

Path 30, Resh, the Sun

Resh is the letter of the Sun card.

“the monsters turned out to be just trees.”

PROFANE

The thing she feared wasn’t real.

INITIATE

This is the second touch from Mark 8. His blind man saw men as trees. She saw trees as monsters. Mark ends on “clearly.” The song is still asking for “the clear.” The Gospel of Thomas, saying 5, gives the rule: “Recognize what is in your sight, and that which is hidden from you will become plain to you.”

The light that clears the woods.

“When the sun came up.”

PROFANE

Morning.

INITIATE

In his chapter on the Moon card, Crowley quotes one line: “There is a budding morrow in midnight.” The gate card already held this sunrise.

THE RETURN

Yesod

The Foundation. The place where the image settles.

In the video she comes out of the woods onto the beach. Another version of her is already standing there. The final picture places the earlier self beside her on the shore she started from.

The earlier self waiting on the shore she started from.

The walk and the return to the same ground.

Back to Malkuth. The floor again. The beach is the same ground.

She built a song that walks a full night, a crash, a sunrise, and a meeting with the earlier self on the same shore. The reader can check every line I used. I sit under the song. Nothing here needs her to have known any of it.

She sees the trees for what they are in the bridge. Why is the last thing she sings still the question?

Love is the law, love under will.

666 – Antichrist
