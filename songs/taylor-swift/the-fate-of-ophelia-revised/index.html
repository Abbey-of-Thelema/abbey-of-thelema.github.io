Taylor Swift, "The Fate of Ophelia." Here's the map.

Malkuth (the tower). "Lived in fantasy," "alone in my tower." Malkuth is the Earth, the final Heh, matter mistaken for reality. Every journey starts here.

Shin, path 31 (Judgement, Trump XX). "I heard you call on the megaphone." Look at the Rider-Waite card: Gabriel blows the trumpet and the dead rise from their graves. That's the call. Then she tells you which path it is: "You're quite the pyro." Shin is Fire. The fire that wakes the dead is the first thing that reaches her in the tower.

Tav, path 32 (Universe) → Yesod. "I might've lingered in purgatory." Yesod is the Moon: images, dreams, the in-between where souls linger. 777 calls Tav the threshold of Yesod. The grave she's raised from is the Moon itself.

Nun, path 24 (Death) → Tiphareth. The scorpions. On Crowley's Atu XIII the scorpion, serpent and eagle are the three forms of Scorpio, and Nun's work is "generation through putrefaction." The sting didn't kill the girl. It killed the fantasy. And Nun climbs to the Sun from Netzach, the sphere of Venus, desire and love. That's the door the profane audience sees: a love story.

Samekh, path 25 (Art) → Tiphareth. Watch the video. She sits in water and the water bursts into fire. That's Atu XIV: fire and water mixed in one vessel. 777: Samekh is "the threshold of Tiphereth." Then the payoff line: "Now I can see it all." Tiphareth's vision is the Vision of the Harmony of Things. Death dissolves, Art mixes, and both open onto the Sun. The fire that started on Shin finishes here.

Gimel, path 13 (Priestess) → Kether. Not walked. The song never crosses the Abyss, and I won't pretend it does. 777 names Tav, Samekh and Gimel as "the three main balanced spiritual experiences in the way of attainment." She took two.
