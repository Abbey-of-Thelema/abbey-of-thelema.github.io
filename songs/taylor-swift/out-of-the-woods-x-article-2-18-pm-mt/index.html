THE DECODE, AS POSTED

Taylor Swift Was Never Singing to a Man — Out of the Woods, Decode #33

Taylor Swift isn’t singing to a man in Out of the Woods. One voice asks the question, and the same voice answers it. At sunrise the pronouns trade places. And at the end of the video, she comes out of the woods and meets her earlier self on the shore.

She is talking to herself. The chorus asks the question. The same voice answers it. The bridge trades the pronouns at each sunrise. The video ends with one person meeting the earlier version of herself on the shore. That is the reading. Everything else is what the surface story uses to hide it.

Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.

Thelema is Greek for Will. It is the fixed direction, not the passing want.

THE KEY

Nearly every old teaching says there is a version of you further down the road, and the call comes from that self. Hear this song as her talking to that self, not to a man. The “you” is the earlier or later version. The “we” is the two of them before they separate and after they meet again.

THE COUCH

“We were lying on your couch. You took a Polaroid of us. The rest of the world was black and white. But we were in screaming color.”

Malkuth

The Kingdom. The ordinary room. Tens sit here.

The Ten of Cups is a man and a woman under a rainbow. The Polaroid is the colored frame. The rest of the world is the black-and-white outside it. Two figures already present in one picture. The album cover is itself a Polaroid. The lyric lives inside the medium of the record.

The Polaroid that makes the rest of the world gray.

THE QUESTION

“Are we out of the woods yet? Are we in the clear yet?”

Path 29, Qoph, the Moon

Waite: the path between the towers is the issue into the unknown. The video places wolves in those woods. 777 gives the power of this path as Bewitchments, Casting Illusions. The letter note: Illusionary is the Initiator of Disorder. Those are the monsters that were trees. Qoph is the back of the head. The song is built on looking at it now and on remembering. Pisces is the sign on the Moon card. Crowley calls it the last stage of winter. This card represents midnight. The song sets its memory in December, at night.

Wolves at the gate. Waite’s own words on the Moon.

The issue into the unknown.

THE SEASON

“Last December. We were built to fall apart. Then fall back together.”

Netzach

The Wheel of Fortune is path 21, the letter Kaph. 777 gives 20 as the number of Kaph. The Wheel is the road that leaves Netzach. Chance and the turn. The Gospel of Thomas, saying 11: “On the day when you were one, you became divided.” The line holds both the break and the return.

THE NECKLACE

“Your necklace hanging from my neck.”

Hod’s picture, shown before the crash carries the song there.

The Devil is path 26. Its path leaves Hod. Waite shows two figures, a man and a woman, chained at the neck. The necklace is already on her.

THE AIRPLANES

“Two paper airplanes flying.”

The Eight of Wands sits at Hod. Waite describes a flight of wands through open country, at hand, on the threshold. The chance they stood is still in the air.

THE CRASH

“Twenty stitches in a hospital room.”

Path 27, Peh, the Tower

Waite’s meaning for the Tower includes “unforeseen catastrophe.” The hidden message printed in the album booklet for this track is “They loved each other recklessly.” The bridge is a crash. The stitches call back to the Wheel. Chance is in the hospital room.

Unforeseen catastrophe.

THE MIRROR

“When you started crying, baby I did too. But when the sun came up I was looking at you.”

Hod

Path 27 lands here.

Psalm 30:2 says “I cried unto thee, and thou hast healed me.” Verse 5 says “weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning.” The bridge has the cry, the healing, then the sunrise. The stitches are the healing. The pronouns have not yet traded.

THE TREES

“The monsters turned out to be just trees.”

Path 30, Resh, the Sun

Mark 8:24-25. His blind man saw men as trees. She saw trees as monsters. Mark ends on “clearly.” The song is still asking for “the clear.” Thomas 5: “Recognize what is in your sight, and that which is hidden from you will become plain to you.”

Crowley on the Moon card: “There is a budding morrow in midnight.” The gate card already held this sunrise. Qoph is Back of head. Resh is Head. The note: The Twins shining forth and playing. In the Sun is the Secret of the Spirit.

The light that clears the woods.

THE TRADE

“When the sun came up you were looking at me.”

The pronouns reverse. She was looking at the other one. Now the other one is looking at her. The same voice. The same shore.

THE RETURN

Yesod

The Foundation.

In the video she comes out of the woods onto the beach. Another version of her is already standing there. The final picture places the earlier self beside her on the shore she started from. The vision of sphere 10 is the Vision of the Holy Guardian Angel or of Adonai. The last thing the bridge says is that the other one is looking at her.

The earlier self waiting on the shore she started from.

The walk and the return to the same ground.

Back to Malkuth. The floor again. The beach is the same ground. The last chorus is the first chorus with somebody standing in it.

She built a song that asks a question, answers it with the same voice, trades the pronouns at sunrise, and ends with one person meeting the earlier version of herself. The craft is tighter than a recovery song needs to be. I sit under the song. Nothing here needs her to have known any of it.

She sees the trees for what they are in the bridge. Why is the last thing she sings still the question?

Love is the law, love under will.
