Antichrist’s DECODE: ANOTHER BRICK IN THE WALL – Pink Floyd
93

Pink Floyd handed you the initiation manual in 1979. You sang along at the stadium and never noticed the Lodge opening behind you.

ANOTHER BRICK IN THE WALL isn’t a protest song about bad teachers. It’s a Middle Pillar initiation ritual. The geometry is locked. Every chant, every “brick,” every command to “leave them kids alone” lands exactly on its station on the Tree. It’s no coincidence.

PROFANE LAYER (what the sheep hear): Just another anti-school, anti-authority banger born from childhood trauma and oppressive education.

ADEPT LAYER (what actually happens): A precise Qabalistic dismantling of the Wall of profane conditioning — the ego barriers, thought-control systems, and Osirian authority that must be recognized and transcended for the Crowned & Conquering Child to rise in the new Aeon. The “bricks” are the incremental ordeals that build the prison until the aspirant declares “we don’t need no education” and the wall begins to crack.

MALKUTH — The Chamber of Reflection
“Daddy’s flown across the ocean / Leaving just a memory / A snapshot in the family album…” / “All in all it was just a brick in the wall.”
Pure Malkuth. The material kingdom of loss and inherited trauma. The first brick is laid by the absent father — the dying Osiris of the old Aeon. The candidate stands in the dark chamber.

Masonic parallel: Chamber of Reflection with skull and hourglass — the aspirant confronts mortality and separation before knocking.

PATH 32 — Tav / The World / The Tyler’s Door
“We don’t need no education / We don’t need no thought control.”
Crossing the 32nd Path. Saturnine nigredo. The declaration of independence from the old Aeon’s entire system of control. The World card’s dancer wears the laurel of completion — the cycle closes. The threshold cannot be uncrossed.

Masonic parallel: Tyler’s door. The password given; the profane world sealed behind you.

YESOD — The Hoodwinked Apprentice
“No dark sarcasm in the classroom / Teachers, leave them kids alone / Hey! Teachers! Leave them kids alone!”
Yesod. Lunar glamour and projected authority. The “dark sarcasm” is the false moonlight of the old teachers — the hoodwink itself. The children’s choir rising is the first direct call to the Holy Guardian Angel — the Babe in the Egg of Blue demanding to be left unhindered.

Masonic parallel: Entered Apprentice, hoodwinked, standing at the threshold.

PATH 25 — Samekh / Art (Temperance) / The Ordeal
“All in all it’s just another brick in the wall / All in all you’re just another brick in the wall.” (repeated, choir)
Path 25. Middle Pillar arrow to Tiphareth. The alchemical furnace. Each “brick” is an ordeal of recognition — the wall is built by you, until you see through it. The repetition is the mantra that dissolves it. Samekh: the prop you surrender when you no longer need it.

Masonic parallel: The three ruffians’ blows on Hiram — the willing ordeal before the raising.

TIPHARETH — The Writing on the Wall
“I don’t need no arms around me / And I don’t need no drugs to calm me / I have seen the writing on the wall / Don’t think I need anything at all.”
Tiphareth attained. Solar rebirth. The Crowned & Conquering Child enthroned. The aspirant who has made Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel needs no external props — no comfort, no chemical hoodwink, no authority to confirm the Work. He has seen the writing on the wall.

Masonic parallel: Hiram raised. The substitute word whispered. The Lodge of the new Aeon opens.
