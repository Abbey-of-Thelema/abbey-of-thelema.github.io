THE DECODE, AS POSTED

93, Greetings & Health!

Respect 🫡 to the order!

Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.

An old woman walks an empty house, talking to her dead husband. That’s what everyone hears in Little Talks by Of Monsters and Men.

That’s not what the song is doing. It’s a map — five steps up an ancient ladder — and the title tells you exactly where it ends. Watch.

🌐 THE HOUSE I don’t like walking around this old and empty house So hold my hand, I’ll walk with you, my dear

PROFANE: A widow in a failing body, afraid of her own home. 
INITIATE: The ordinary world is the bottom House on the Tree of Life — the one almost nobody ever leaves. She can’t cross it alone, because one vision is granted at the bottom floor: the Holy Guardian Angel. The hand she reaches for is not a memory.

⚡ THE BURIAL Soon it will be over and buried with our past

PROFANE: Grief. The old life goes into the ground. 
INITIATE: Saturn’s gate. In this system the grave is the exit — the burial opens the climb.

🌙 THE TRICKS Don’t listen to a word I say The screams all sound the same

PROFANE: She’s losing her mind — or is she? 
INITIATE: The Moon sphere.
Eliphas Levi, 1856: the dead appear as reflections in the Astral Light, answering “never with a voice which really strikes the ears — for how should a shadow speak?”

Every cry sounds identical because a mirror reflects. It doesn’t speak. The test is telling light from reflection.

🏹 THE SHIP This ship will carry our bodies safe to shore

PROFANE: Comfort. We’ll make it through together. 
INITIATE: One path runs from Moon to Sun, and the old books draw it as an Arrow. One vessel, two voices — man and woman singing as a single line — aimed at one shore while truth shifts around it.

☀️ THE MEETING Just let me go, we’ll meet again soon I’ll see you when I fall asleep

PROFANE: A goodbye, and a hope.
 INITIATE: The Sun. The dead beloved is the Sacrificed God of Tiphareth. Crowley’s private diary of this exact attainment prays: “give unto Thy beloved in sleep.” Levi’s farewell to the beloved dead promises “the day when we shall awake together.” The bridge isn’t a goodbye. It’s the appointment.

🔄 THE RETURN The song ends where it started — in the house. But the last chorus isn’t the same as the first. She carried something back down.

THE SEAL The destination of this entire climb has a formal name: the Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel.
The song is called Little Talks.
And one more thing.

That “Hey” you’ve screamed with thousands of strangers? It’s the Hebrew letter Heh — the letter of the Star — sung twice, like the two Hehs in the four-letter Name of God.

Mother and Daughter, answering each other across the veil.
