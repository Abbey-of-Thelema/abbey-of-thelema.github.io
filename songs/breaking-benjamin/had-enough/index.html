Antichrist Decoding: BREAKING BENJAMIN –
HAD ENOUGH

PROFANE LAYER (what the sheep hear): A bitter takedown of a greedy ex-bandmate (or “Satan”/the system) who milked everything for all it was worth. Classic rock-radio catharsis about getting screwed and finally snapping back.

ADEPT LAYER (what actually happens): A precise Qabalistic dismantling of the profane ego — the Osirian “greedy little bastard” who must be confronted, burned, and transcended so the Crowned and Conquering Child can rise in the new Aeon. The song is the aspirant’s declaration that the old Aeon’s prison is over.

MALKUTH — Chamber of Reflection
“Milk it for all its worth / Make sure you get there first / The apple of your eye / The rotten core inside / We are all prisoners / Things couldn’t get much worse / I’ve had it up to here / You know your end is near”

Pure Malkuth. The material kingdom as prison. The “apple of your eye” is the profane Bride’s false Eden — beautiful on the surface, rotten at the core (Assiah’s Princess Scale: Citrine, Olive, Russet, Black). The candidate stands in the dark chamber, surrounded by the rusted machinery of the old Aeon, declaring the threshold.

Masonic parallel: Chamber of Reflection — skull, hourglass, and the final warning before the knock.

PATH 32 — Tav / The World / The Tyler’s Door
“You know your end is near” / “You had to have it all / Well, have you had enough?”

Crossing the 32nd Path. Saturnine nigredo. The World card’s dancer has completed the cycle; the old Aeon’s greed is sealed behind the Tyler’s door forever. The question “have you had enough?” is Tav’s own sickle reaping the profane harvest.

Masonic parallel: Tyler’s door. One word grants passage — the profane world is now locked out.

YESOD — The Hoodwinked Apprentice
“Intoxicated eyes / No longer live that life / You should have learned by now”

Yesod. Lunar glamour and projected desire. The “intoxicated eyes” are the hoodwink itself — false moonlight of the old ego’s addictions. The aspirant sees through the reflection and refuses to live that life any longer.

Masonic parallel: Entered Apprentice, hoodwinked at the threshold, confronting the rough ashlar.

PATH 25 — Samekh / Art (Temperance) / The Ordeal
“I’ll burn this whole world down / I need some peace of mind / No fear of what’s behind / You think you’ve won this fight / You’ve only lost your mind”

Path 25. The Arrow of the Middle Pillar. Alchemical furnace. The old world is set ablaze in the nigredo-to-rubedo transition. Samekh surrenders the prop; the candidate walks through the fire without fear. The greedy ego has “lost its mind” — the ordeal is complete.

Masonic parallel: The three ruffians’ blows on Hiram — the willing destruction before the raising.

TIPHARETH — Hiram Raised / The Dawn Long Overdue
Bridge: “Hold me down / I will live again / Pull me out / I will break it in / Hold me down / Better in the end”
Chorus payoff: “When all is said and done / I will be the one / To leave you in your misery / And hate what you’ve become”
Outro: “Heaven help you / Heaven help you”

Tiphareth attained. Solar rebirth. The Crowned and Conquering Child rises. “I will live again” is the Lion’s Paw grip — Hiram raised from the grave of the old Aeon. The aspirant leaves the greedy bastard (the dying Osiris) in his misery while the new Sun dawns. “Heaven help you” is the final solar benediction to the profane self left behind.

Masonic parallel: Raising of Hiram. The substitute word is spoken. The Lodge of the new Aeon opens.

TAROT

• The Sun (Tiphareth)
• The Tower (burn this whole world down — optional shadow)

Inherited prison → threshold declaration → lunar confrontation → alchemical burning → solar resurrection.
