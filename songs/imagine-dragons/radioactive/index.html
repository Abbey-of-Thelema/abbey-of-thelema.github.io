🔮 ANTICHRIST DECODE #29: "Radioactive" by Imagine Dragons

Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.

Picture it first: grey ash on the ground, and someone standing up out of it.

The song opens there: "waking up to ash and dust." That is the floor of the Tree, Malkuth: the bottom rung of the Tree of Life, the Kabbalah's ladder from the ground up to God, where everything ends up burnt down to what it really is.

Then comes "the apocalypse." Read it as the end of an old self's world, not of bodies. Nobody is being told to hurt anyone; the only thing that dies here is a way of living that stopped working. Crowley's reference book 777 puts it in five words: "Resurrection is hidden in Death."

"I'm waking up." "Feel it in my bones." "Welcome to the new age."

That is the Judgement card, gate Shin 31: the first door up off that bottom rung, path 31, marked by the Hebrew letter Shin, the letter of fire. Waite's tarot deck shows the dead rising from their tombs. 777 calls the path "the fire of the Last Judgement (ATU XX)". Crowley renamed that card the Aeon.

"I'm waking up" is the same call Liber AL II:34, Crowley's Book of the Law, makes: "But ye, o my people, rise up & awake!"

The ash is not the end of the story. It is the place you stand up from.

None of this claims the band meant any of it. It is one way of reading the song, nothing more.
