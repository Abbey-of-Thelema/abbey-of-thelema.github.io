She thinks she wrote a breakup song. She wrote a clean Middle Pillar ascent from Malkuth to Tiphareth. The doubled “baby” calls the Child in the Sun. The “hit” is the arrow striking home.

MALKUTH — The Broken Kingdom

Oh, baby, baby

How was I supposed to know

That somethin’ wasn’t right here?

PROFANE: She realizes too late the relationship was already over.

INITIATE: Malkuth wakes to the fact that the kingdom she stands in is already broken. The natural body registers that something fundamental is off.

PATH 32 (Tav) — The Binding Gate

Oh, baby, baby

I shouldn’t have let you go

And now you’re out of sight, yeah

PROFANE: Regret. She let them go and now they’re gone.

INITIATE: Tav, the Saturn gate. The separation is already the door.

YESOD — The Lunar Crisis

My loneliness is killin’ me

When I’m not with you, I lose my mind

PROFANE: She’s unraveling without them.

INITIATE: Yesod. The reflected light. Without the direct solar current from Tiphareth the Nephesh fragments in the mirror. This is the necessary crisis before the arrow can fly.

PATH 25 (Samekh) — The Arrow

Show me how you want it to be

Tell me, baby, ’cause I need to know now

Give me a sign

PROFANE: She begs for clarity and direction.

INITIATE: Samekh, the arrow from Yesod to Tiphareth. She asks to be shown the aim and volunteers for the test.

TIPHARETH — The Child and the Hit

Hit me, baby, one more time

The reason I breathe is you

Oh, pretty baby

PROFANE: She pleads for one more chance and says they are everything.

INITIATE: Tiphareth. The doubled “baby” invokes the Child (777: child, majestic king, crucified god) — the Holy Guardian Angel. The “hit” is the Samekh arrow striking home. It is the strike that activates the passive into the active, the natural body into the spiritual body (1 Cor 15:44), and makes her male so she becomes a living spirit (Gospel of Thomas 114). The rod of correction (Heb 12:6). The ordeal that kills the old form so the new can stand in Beauty.

The Seal

Malkuth registers the broken kingdom. Tav sets the binding at the gate. Yesod forces the confrontation with the messy reflection and the loneliness that kills. Samekh demands the arrow be aimed at one thing only. Tiphareth answers as the Child. The hit is not external punishment. It is the arrow finally finding its mark. The lower dies so the higher can stand. The name that cannot be cut off is given at the center (Isa 56:5).
