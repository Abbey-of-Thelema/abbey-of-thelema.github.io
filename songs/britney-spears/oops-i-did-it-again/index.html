OOPS!… I DID IT AGAIN. AntiChrist Decodes symbolism of Britney Spears.

Route: Malkuth → Path 32 (Tav) → Yesod → Path 25 (Samekh) → Tiphareth → Return to Malkuth

🌐 MALKUTH — Adonai ha-Aretz
777 Key 10. Nephesh — the sensing soul.

Lyrics
“I think I did it again…”
“…to lose all my senses / That is just so typically me.”

Video
Red Mars. Buried stone uncovered. Lowered in a spiky sun-like form. Giant unblinking eye.

PROFANE
A girl admitting she keeps repeating the same pattern and losing herself in it.

INITIATE
Malkuth is the bottom sphere — the Kingdom, the material world. Mars also carries Gevurah: red, fire, destructive force. The verse is Nephesh naming its own nature. The opening eye matches Konx Om Pax (1907): an eye “without an eyelid… that could never go to sleep.”

⚡ PATH 32 (TAV) — The Universe

777 Key 32: Tav, The Universe card.

Lyrics
“Oops I did it again, I played with your heart / Got lost in the game…”

PROFANE
She did it again and doesn’t care.

INITIATE
Confession that opens the door out of Malkuth. “Got lost in the game” names The Universe card directly.

🌙 YESOD — Levanah
777 Key 9: Foundation, Sphere of Luna.
Lyrics
“You see, my problem is this / I’m dreaming away…”
“…wishing that heroes they truly existed.”

Video
Overhead shot: girl centered on a glowing white disc, eight dancers around her, arms rotating in a wheel-like pattern.

PROFANE
Girl admitting she lives in fantasy.

INITIATE
She names the sphere outright: “I’m dreaming away.” Konx Om Pax (1907) describes the Ninth House almost exactly like this shot. Eight dancers + one central figure = nine. The rotating arms form the fylfot — 777 assigns it to Kether as “whirling energy, the initiation of all magical force.”

🏹 PATH 25 (SAMEKH) — The Arrow

777 Key 25: Samekh, Temperance/Art.

Lyrics
“All aboard!”
“…isn’t this…?”
“I went down and got it for ya”
“Aw, you shouldn’t have.”

Video
She flips through the air to the astronaut. After the gift she returns with almost no reaction. Earth watches through screens.

PROFANE
The famous Titanic necklace joke.

INITIATE
Samekh is the Arrow. She performs it with her body. The lines compress the Hymn of the Pearl — retrieval from the deep. She accepts the gift and returns to the mediated world carrying what was gained.

☀️ TIPHARETH — Shemesh, YHVH Eloah va-

777 Key 6. Contains Da’ath — Knowledge. Holds the Child, the Majestic King, and the Dying God.

Lyrics
Final stacked choruses, closing on:
“I’m not that innocent.”

PROFANE
Same chorus returns.

INITIATE
Tiphareth is the central sphere of Beauty and the Sun. Repetition here becomes liturgy. The song has been addressing “baby” the entire time — the call to this sphere, the Holy Guardian Angel. She stands in the one sphere whose listed power is the Vision of the Harmony of Things and the Mysteries of the Crucifixion. Red crossed-arms pose = Osiris Risen. White flat pose = Osiris Slain.

🔄 RETURN TO MALKUTH

Lyrics
The final chorus — identical to the first.

Video
She returns to the mediated world. Earth continues watching through screens. Ordinary life resumes.

PROFANE
Everything goes back to normal.

INITIATE
The climb was never an exit. The final chorus is the return — the same words as the opening, now inhabited by what was gained. Konx Om Pax (“The Wake World”) ends the same way: the narrator remains “Lola,” only renamed in degree (“Wide-awake” instead of “Daydream”). She is never removed from the world she started in. Illumination descends back into the body that never left Malkuth.

An eye that cannot sleep.
Eight dancers around a central figure with the oldest wheel-symbol spinning above them.
A girl who flies the Arrow, accepts the gift, and returns to the mediated world carrying what was gained.

One chorus that means something different the second time it is heard — because the same person is now standing in it.
