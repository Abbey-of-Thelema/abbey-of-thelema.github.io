ANTICHRIST DECODE: GHOST — SQUARE HAMMER

You’ve heard Square Hammer a thousand times. The refrain was never the candidate pleading. It is the Angel’s trumpet.

PATH 31 — SHIN — JUDGMENT / THE AEON — FIRE

“You call on me to solve a crooked rhyme / As I’m closing in / Imposing on your slumber / You call on me as bells begin to chime”

Bells chiming, a closing-in, a sleeper disturbed — this is the trumpet over the graves. Shin, the fire-letter; Judgment, the Aeon. Only three paths stand in Malkuth: Tav (World/Saturn), Shin (Judgment/Fire), Qoph (Moon/Pisces). Square Hammer takes the door of fire. The voice is the summons calling the corpse out. The candidate is the dead being raised.

PROFANE: a hook repeating.
INITIATE: the resurrection-trumpet sounding three times, each peal raising the candidate one degree. The repetition is the ascent.

PATH 27 — PEH — THE TOWER — MARS

“Mating in the night / ’Neath heavens torn asunder / You call on me to solve a crooked rhyme”

Peh, the mouth; Mars, the lightning. The false firmament is struck open. The crooked rhyme of the old formula cannot be straightened from inside the crooked tower.

PROFANE: gothic storm and riddle.
INITIATE: the summoned candidate’s structure broken open before the work begins.

PATH 26 — AYIN — THE DEVIL — CAPRICORN

“Are you on the square? Are you on the level? Are you ready to swear right here, right now before the devil / That you’re on the square? That you’re on the level? That you’re ready to stand right here, right now?”

Ayin, the eye; Capricorn, the goat at the gate. Baphomet — opposites reconciled. The oath is sworn in the present: right here, right now. The chorus asks you to swear then asks if you are ready to stand. The chains were always loose.

PROFANE: daring you to swear to the Devil.
INITIATE: the lower sworn true to the higher at the goat’s threshold. The stand is the passing.

THE THRESHOLD — THE SHRINE

“Hiding from the light / Sacrificing nothing / Still, you call on me for entrance to the shrine”

The shrine is Tiphareth. The candidate hides and offers nothing — yet the call sounds. The door names its price. No more hiding. No empty sacrifice. Only full entrance.

PROFANE: wanting in without paying.
INITIATE: the Fire still calls, but the candidate has not answered in the currency the gate accepts.

PATH 24 — NUN — DEATH — SCORPIO → TIPHARETH

“Hammering the nails / Into a sacred coffin / You call on me for powers clandestine”

Nun, the fish; Scorpio. The maul finishes Hiram; behind it, Set sealing Osiris. One image primary, the other its echo. The old formula is delivered into the coffin. The powers that were hidden become the only ones that remain.

PROFANE: finest horror imagery.
INITIATE: the sacrifice paid. Death delivers the candidate to the center.

THE RAISING — TIPHARETH

“That you’re ready to stand right here, right now? / Right here, right now”

Judgment opened the gate. Death delivered him to the center. The call and the arrival are the same Fire. The corpse that lay in Malkuth now stands in Tiphareth — Beauty, Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel.

PROFANE: the climax — get up, right now.
INITIATE: the raising. Tiphareth attained.

THE THESIS

Three times the call sounds. The profane hear a hook. The initiate hears one trumpet calling a single soul up through every degree until what answers is the raised version of the one who was called.

The Antichrist is the force that answers the trumpet — the part of you that lay dead in Malkuth and now stands. It takes the hammer that killed it and holds it upright.

I am that force. You called on me.

This pattern does not stop with Ghost.
