THE DECODE, AS POSTED

ANTICHRIST DECODE
PISTOL ANNIES – Best Years of My Life

This is a 5°=6° working sung as a country ballad. Three women recorded the path of Samekh, and it has been on repeat for years, unheard.

Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.

MALKUTH – ASSIAH – THE TEN-CENT TOWN
I’m gonna mix a drink and try to drown this worthlessness.
Call Mom and tell her I’m alright.
Well he don’t love me but he ain’t gone yet.

PROFANE: She lives in a small town with a husband who doesn’t love her.

INITIATE: The tenth sphere in Assiah. The spark buried under citrine, olive, russet and black — the four elements priced at ten cents. 777 places the Vision of the Holy Guardian Angel in Malkuth, not Tiphareth. The Angel is first glimpsed from the floor. She does not yet know what she is looking at. The town is not where she lives. It is the price she accepted for herself the day she stopped looking up. Thomas, Saying 3.

PATH 32 – TAU – SATURN
I was looking forward to
Staying here forever
’Cause you asked me to
Didn’t think that I could do better
So I settled down
In this ten-cent town

PROFANE: She settled down because he asked her to.

INITIATE: ת. Lead. The sickle. Indigo. The binding of the Nephesh into form. She signed the lease on the shell because he asked and because she no longer believed anything above was worth the climb. Tau does not judge the binding. It records it. The vow made in the dream that the dream was final. First matter, owned without excuse. The Great Work begins here or it does not begin.

YESOD – THE FALSE MOON
I picked a good day for a recreational Percocet.
I’ve got an itch to just get high.
I’ve got the hankering for intellectual emptiness.
I’ve got the need to ease my mind.
I’ll watch some reruns on the TV set.

PROFANE: She takes a pill and watches TV to feel better.

INITIATE: The ninth sphere. Foundation. Seat of the Nephesh. 777 gives Yesod the Vision of the Machinery of the Universe — and she is watching reruns. The same loop, on schedule. The pill is lunar. The screen is lunar. The body beside her is lunar: reflection without source. Liber AL I:3. The itch is the corrupted memory of the Cup. A star that forgets its own light does not stop needing light. It borrows. The false moon does not lie. It has nothing else to offer.

PATH 25 – SAMEKH – ART
It’s about to break me.

PROFANE: She’s about to have a breakdown.

INITIATE: ס. The prop. The only path from Yesod to Tiphareth and the road Crowley named Liber Samekh for a reason. The prop must break for the path to open. Art: fire poured into water, water into fire — and she is the substance being tempered. Sagittarius. The arrow loosed blind through the dark night. The aspirant does not aim. The aspirant is the arrow. The breaking is not pathology. It is the 5°=6° crisis arriving exactly on time.

TIPHARETH – SOL – THE GOLDEN YEARS
These are the best years of my life
These are the best years of my life
These are the best years of my life

PROFANE: She’s being sarcastic. These are obviously not the best years of her life.

INITIATE: תִּפְאֶרֶת. The Sun. The Son. The Mysteries of the Crucifixion and the Vision of the Harmony of Things. The profane tongue already encoded the formula: the best years of a life are the golden years, and gold is the metal of Sol. The song opens in Saturn’s lead and closes in the Sun’s gold. The path between is Art. She is not coping. She is prophesying her own attainment. Adeptus Minor is not reached when the town improves. It is reached when the Sun rises in the middle of it.

The profane and the initiate hear the same eight words. They are not hearing the same song.

One star in sight, from a ten-cent town.

THE SEAL
Lead in the town, moon in the vein,
prop snaps clean in the dark-fired rain.
Arrow flies blind where the breaking is —
gold in the heart. Adonai. Mine.

Love is the law, love under will.
